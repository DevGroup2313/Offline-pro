INKWELL v7.52: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 24 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p51.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's
signing key. The measurement data is never in the repository and never
trained on. SECURITY.txt stays private. Since 24 October the private source
lives on the "inkwell" branch of DevGroup2313/Offline-pro (private, at the
writer's word); yabban-maker/inkwell-fire is disconnected and receives
nothing.

WHY 7.52
--------
The writer's standing request: the offline corrector and editor pushed
further, at a doctoral standard, teaching while it helps, with no model
needed. After 7.51 the work took the remaining silences by type, the
spelling fix first, then the small deterministic fixes, and made the
teaching particular: the lesson of a note is now the word or the sentence
itself, not the family's general line.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. The guided tour shows the teaching (src/inkwell.html, TOUR): "The rule in
   a breath" after the accepted fix (the line at the foot held while the
   card is up), "Your patterns" by family with practice, "Learning as you
   go" in the Focus chapter (My way of working opened, The rule after a fix
   ringed). The Guide's "Learn as you go" row and the Studio label name the
   supports.
2. One receipt at the foot. The Undo chip stood over the shelf's "+ New
   document" button; it stands where the message stands, and when a
   message follows at once Undo rides the message (toast, showUndoChip).
3. The spelling fix, measured (src/lib/spell_core.js; tools/grammar/
   README.md, "the spelling fix"): the level cost of a candidate's rarity
   widened; a long word two slips from its only very common candidate gets
   the fix; a run of slips is not a phrase of another language; a
   neighbouring key costs about what a letter left out costs; a slip at
   the first letter costs more. Guards from the correct sets: a detached
   prefix, a term glossed in brackets or defined by its sentence, loanwords
   without accents, the small words of other languages inside names, four-
   letter words, rare words, a short capital at a sentence's start. The
   annotator's own spelling fix: 82 of 129 on the tuning half (was 68).
   Every spelling card teaches the word itself (INKSPELL.lesson: the
   classic words' lines, else the tricky part read off the slip).
4. The small deterministic fixes (learner_core.js, gaps_core.js; README,
   "the small deterministic fixes"): the comma after a connective whatever
   follows; the comma before an instruction after an opening clause; the
   comma before "but" with a noun phrase; the space after a sentence's
   stop, with the capital; the paragraph's missing full stop when another
   paragraph follows; "in to" after a verb of motion; a role noun's
   article; "congratulations on", "a beginner at", "smile at", "pray to"
   God. Two faults in the page's pipeline found by the measurement: the
   gate dropped "i'm", and an old rule for the missing space blocked the
   library's fix. A correctness note now takes the place of a style note
   on the same words (inkYield), which also lets through "cannot", "is a
   very beautiful place", "a lot of".
5. The plural subject's verb ("my friends likes" to "like") and the
   lessons built from the sentence itself for agreement, number and the
   article (refine_core.js sentenceLesson), on the card, in the line at
   the foot (read before the page changes, acceptOne e._L) and in the
   practice's answer. An insertion at the head of a note that keeps its
   first word stands beside it (dedupe, inkYield), and Fix all applies the
   longer fix first.
6. Your patterns counts a fix under its family (patFamily): the gaps'
   families as before, and for the page's and the learner library's own
   notes the family their reason belongs to (The verb agrees with its
   subject; Singular or plural after a quantity; A missing article; The
   article: "a" or "an"; A comma after an opener or between clauses;
   Capitals; A word and its preposition; The verb's form after a modal,
   "do", "have" or "to"; One word or two, and the spaces), each with its
   lesson (refine_core.js LESSON). The counts kept under each wording of a
   rule fold into their families once (inkFoldStats). One sentence from
   last time names the family.
7. The corrector read against 40 published articles (247,426 words, the
   7.47 set) in the scholarly register: 554 correctness notes, 408 with a
   fix, became 409 and 268, each misfiring shape guarded with a test
   (tools/grammar/README.md, "under scrutiny on published prose, again"):
   abstract nouns given an article ("about epistemic agency"); "only",
   "first" and "best" used as adverbs given "the"; a title's capital
   ("Scent of a Woman", "Leech and Short"); identity capitals ("Black
   patients"); the noun "will"; a list's "(i)"; "the The Sanitary…";
   plural objects taken for subjects ("preferences and values is");
   transcript codes and bracketed sources at a paragraph's end; a species
   after its genus initial; learned terms ("analepsis"); -er nouns from
   verbs ("funders"); two words run together split ("empiricalevidence").
   The learner sets did not move; the right fixes stayed.

Whole page, ERRANT F0.5, from 7.51: W&I tuning half 0.291 to 0.311,
report half 0.262 to 0.274, CoNLL-2014 0.272 to 0.278; correct prose
changed 4.5% to 4.1% (tuning) and 4.8% to 4.5% (report).

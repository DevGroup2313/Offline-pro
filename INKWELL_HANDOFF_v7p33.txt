INKWELL v7.33: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 4 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p32.txt and earlier for
everything before this.

Look for "v7.33" beside the date at the top.

YOUR REPORT (with screenshots from the Mac app)
-----------------------------------------------
- Rewrite and "Use this" changed text that was not selected, and added text
  that did not belong; the in-app models made writing worse on the iPhone,
  iPad, Fire and Mac; selecting text painted the whole page and could cover
  the edit buttons; the corrector flagged far too much. You asked for the
  weak models to be removed and for better ones.

1. REWRITES REPLACED THE WRONG TEXT (DOCUMENT DAMAGE): FIXED
------------------------------------------------------------
Seen in your screenshots: the Introduction was selected, but the Concise
and Shorten versions were of text from a later section, and the document
lost about 107 words ("public be3what the claim..."). Cause, reproduced in a
test browser: the page repaints its text whenever the suggestions change;
the repaint destroyed the browser's selection, and the app then read a
wrong position (Chrome: the whole page; Safari, which the Mac, iPhone and
iPad apps use, can re-anchor the selection on other text of the same
length). The text taken for a rewrite, and the text "Use this" replaced,
were then not the text you had selected.
Changes:
- Every repaint notes the selection and puts it back exactly.
- A selection position that cannot be read is treated as unknown, never as
  "the end of the page".
- The rewrite target is checked against the text visibly selected; if they
  differ, the visible text is found in the document; if it cannot be found,
  nothing is rewritten.
- "Use this", accepting a suggestion, and "Fix the selection" now check that
  the text at the stored position is still exactly the original text. If it
  moved, it is found again; if it is gone, nothing is replaced.
- "Fix the selection" could replace the WHOLE document with the corrected
  selection if the selection was lost while the model worked. It can no
  longer replace anything without a selection.
- A rewrite now needs a selection; it never rewrites the whole document.
Tested: drag, triple-click and double-click selections on a 5,486-word
document across pages, before and after repaints; "Use this" replaced
exactly the selected paragraph and nothing else; after text was inserted
above, it found the paragraph at its new place; with the text gone, it
replaced nothing.
NOT tested in Safari itself (not available in this sandbox). Your
documents: the version list (File) keeps earlier versions; "The Difficulty
Was the Point ... 5429 words" in your list looks like the undamaged copy.

2. THE IN-APP MODELS ARE REMOVED
--------------------------------
GECToR and the 0.5-billion-parameter Pro model are no longer offered on any
device. On start, Inkwell switches them off and deletes their downloaded
files (Scriba's Whisper files stay). Rewriting and drafting need a real
writing model, which runs on the laptop; see the next round.

3. THE RULES ENGINE: FAR FEWER FALSE ALARMS
-------------------------------------------
Measured on 3,000 correct Wikipedia sentences (62,489 words) and the 748
learner sentences of JFLEG:
                               red "grammar" flags     clarity + style notes
                               on correct text         on correct text
  before 7.33                        316                    2,168
  7.33                               100 *                  hidden unless
                                                            asked for
  * nearly all real faults in that text (stray spaces before punctuation,
    "1990's", "is comprised of"); in the Scholarly register 101.
On the learner sentences, 75% of red flags now fall on words the human
correctors changed (54% before).
Faults fixed, each seen on correct text: "may" (the verb) taken for the
month; "an FBI" corrected to "a FBI"; "an honorary" to "a honorary"; an
ellipsis "..." corrected to one full stop; "In addition to" given a comma;
a comma demanded after "However," that already had one; "lowercase
sentence start" after initials and abbreviations (M.A., U.S., i.e., e.g.);
"Capital I" inside foreign words and in "i.e."; accented words cut in half;
"there was 1 individual"; "well known" (not before a noun) hyphenated;
names like "Bang Bang" called doubled words; empty red marks. Comma
splices, "which/that" notes, dangling openers and "spell out a numeral"
moved from grammar to clarity or style.
Clarity and style notes (weak words, passive voice, repeated words, long
sentences) are hidden unless you press "Show clarity and style notes": on
correct text they were mostly noise.

4. SELECTING TEXT
-----------------
- Safari painted the space between selected lines and the gaps between
  pages in the selection colour. The page blocks now have no selection
  colour; only the words are highlighted. (Not verified in Safari itself.)
- The floating edit buttons for a selection used a position estimate from
  the old single-field editor, which could put them over the toolbar. They
  now sit just above the selection, or below it when there is no room, and
  never over the toolbar or the selected words.

ABOUT "FULL PARITY WITH GRAMMARLY"
----------------------------------
Stated plainly: Grammarly's corrections come from large models running on
Grammarly's servers, trained on very large amounts of edited text. No model
that fits inside an app window on a phone or tablet comes near that; the
measurements in 7.32 showed it. What Inkwell can do offline is a precise
rules engine (this round), plus a real writing model on your laptop shared
over Wi-Fi, and Apple's own on-device model on iPhones and iPads that have
it (next round). Any comparison will be stated as measured results, not as
parity.

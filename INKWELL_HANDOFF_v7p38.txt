INKWELL v7.38: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 10 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p36.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.37.

Look for "v7.38" beside the date at the top. If it is not there, the device
is not running 7.38.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL
------------------
On every device: File, Export library, and keep the file. Each new build
installs over the old one and keeps your documents, but uninstalling
deletes them. Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package holds the
app's signing key.

WHAT CAME IN 7.38
-----------------
1. The seals, pressed into the wax. The main seal keeps the pen in the
   inkwell, navy by day and gold by night, with a gilded collar and base,
   lit as if struck into the wax. Schedae's seal is a quill writing on
   rolled parchment, on every Schedae button. The app icons are redrawn
   from the new seal; on the iPhone and iPad home screen the seal sits on
   its own cream (#f2e7cc).
2. Schedae: "Send to Tabula" beside "Send to the page"; six note colours
   that suit Inkwell by day and by night (Foolscap, the first, is the
   default); every colour and every symbol button names itself on hover,
   above the button, in the page's own colours.
3. Right-click Paste works with text copied from other apps, through each
   device's own clipboard (Mac and Windows app, Fire, iPhone and iPad).
4. Clepsydra in Inkwell's colours; a 3D footer fold button; the guided
   tour's buttons are 3D and animated; the heavy square shadow behind the
   side symbol buttons is gone. Every sentence and title starts with a
   capital; an interface text sweep fixed grammar, plurals and stray
   debug words.
5. Rewrite with a prompt works with no model. Every library prompt, and the
   writer's own prompts where their words say what they ask:
   - rule rewrites, each change listed with its reason; new modes:
     Simplify, One idea per sentence, Active voice (plain past, the doer
     named after "by"), Neutral, Hedged to the evidence; Correct only
     (grammar rules and spelling, with each fix's lesson);
   - a coach for what rules cannot write (Show, don't tell; Signpost;
     Synthesis; Topic sentence; Method; Abstract; Rhythm; Expand; Warm;
     Diplomatic; and others): it quotes the words, says how, gives a marked
     example, and checks the writer's own try (Your turn, Check mine, Use
     mine; the writer's version is recorded as typed by the writer).
   src/lib/rewrite_core.js and src/lib/coach_core.js; tests in
   tools/rewrite (230 and 38 checks).
6. Another computer's model. A laptop (MacBook Pro, Windows) can use the
   Mac mini's model over the Wi-Fi: Engine, "Another computer's model".
   This computer's own model stands in whenever the Mac mini does not
   answer. Tablets and phones: "Your computer's model". The shared port now
   offers /api/ask too. Inkwell must be open on the Mac mini.
7. The item on the Desktop. On the Mac it was the installer disk the .dmg
   mounts; Inkwell now ejects each installer disk holding this version or
   an older one when it opens from Applications (never a newer one, never
   forced). On Windows the Desktop shortcut is optional and unticked.

MEASURED IN 7.38
----------------
- Grammar (rules and spelling), held out: W&I report half 67.6% / 4.3% /
  F0.5 0.173; CoNLL-2014 80.5% / 5.8% / 0.224; correct Wikipedia changed
  3.5%. All unchanged from 7.37: the 7.38 rule fixes were to style notes.
- The new rewrite modes on 600 Wikipedia sentences, every change read by
  hand: Active voice changed 3, One idea per sentence 6, Simplify 22,
  Neutral 5, Hedged 0; all keep the meaning after five faults were fixed.

NOT TESTED ON YOUR DEVICES
--------------------------
Everything above was tested in a browser here (Chromium), by day, by
night and at phone size, with stand-ins for the desktop window host, the
Fire and iPad bridges, two servers on a pretend home network, and hdiutil.
Nothing has been tried on the real Mac mini, MacBook Pro, Windows laptop,
Fire tablet, iPhone or iPad. The iPhone clipboard code (Swift) and the
disk ejecting on the Mac can only be proven on those devices.

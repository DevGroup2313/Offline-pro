INKWELL v7.49: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 19 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p48.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.49.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's
signing key. The measurement data (Write & Improve, LOCNESS, CoNLL-2014,
JFLEG, the published articles, the thesis sample, the 100,000-word test
thesis) is never in the repository and never trained on. The word counts
from published English used in the research below are not in it either.

WHY 7.49
--------
The writer asked for Grammarly's offline features, with no model, and more:
a personal dictionary that takes rare words, less common spellings and
names instead of marking them again and again; capitals and symbols kept
one way through a document; and the other features seen in Grammarly's
screens (goals, performance, a filter by kind, drafting and grading). Then,
after seeing 7.49 on the screen: the saved time and the footer's fold
button in the footer's right corner; the chosen filter chip legible by
night, with the same face as its neighbours; and every button and card
lifting and sinking like the rest. The writer approved the designs shown.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Sentence checks (src/inkwell.html, the rules engine).
   - A comma splice offers its fix (a full stop and a capital) and names
     the semicolon and a joining word. An opening clause ("Even when you
     can, it"), a relative aside, "that is" and a conditional inside the
     clause are not taken for splices. On 40 published articles: 26 marks
     before, 6 after, all six true splices.
   - A capital at the head of a sentence, after ! and ?, and at a
     paragraph's start; a line that carries on the one before (verse, a
     list after a colon), titles, a word with an inner capital and an
     abbreviation are left alone.
   - Days and months carry their capital as the fix; "march", "may" and
     "august" only where they stand as a date. Peoples, languages, places
     and feasts in lowercase are capitalised ("arabic numerals" and "french
     fries" left alone). "tv" becomes "TV".
   - tools/grammar/sentence_test.mjs: 24 cases in the built page (copy it
     to a folder where the playwright package resolves; INKWELL_ROOT names
     the repository).
2. Grade and draft without a model.
   - Grade (src/lib/grade_core.js, INKGRADE; Coach, Grade against a
     rubric): each rubric line is read for what it asks and marked 1 to 5
     from what can be counted on the page (paragraphs and their openings,
     signposts, a stated position, reasons, objections weighed, citations,
     quotations, figures, examples, the introduction and the conclusion;
     clarity, vocabulary and register from INKMARK), with what was counted,
     "Show me" and one way to raise it. A line that cannot be counted
     becomes a question the writer answers with a mark of their own. The
     writing model is a choice, not the default. The rubric persists.
   - Draft with me (src/lib/draft_core.js, INKDRAFT; the Draft button): an
     argument paragraph, an introduction, a conclusion, an email or a
     summary of a source, built from the writer's answers to a few
     questions; Inkwell adds only the joining words, by register. With a
     writing model loaded, "Ask the writing model instead" opens the old
     drafting (composeModel).
   - Your patterns (Instruments): the list showed a pattern's first letter
     as its name; it shows the name and the number. Accepted fixes keep
     the writer's own sentence (six to a pattern), and "Practise with your
     own sentences" asks for the mend.
3. Your own dictionary and notes that stay dismissed.
   - "+ Add" on a row for a word the checker does not know (shown when the
     row has no fix), and "Add to dictionary" on every mark about a word.
     Studio, Rules, Your dictionary takes typed words and removes one. The
     writer's words join the checker (INKSPELL.addWords, dropWord) and are
     offered as the correction of their slips ("Gadamar" to "Gadamer").
   - The rule cache keys on the dictionary (an added word was still marked
     by the cached consistency check).
   - A dismissed note stays dismissed in its document (kept by its kind,
     its words and the words round it, in S.dismissed); Studio, Rules can
     show them again.
4. One way throughout (src/lib/consist_core.js, INKCONSIST): a term's
   capitals and hyphen, quotation marks, apostrophes, dashes, ellipses,
   number ranges, large numbers and a unit's space, each marked where the
   document departs from the way it uses most (two thirds of the uses at
   least), with that way as the fix. Names, titles, headings and references
   are not counted; a common word is never raised to a capital. A document
   that mixes US and British spellings is fixed to the one it uses most.
   On 40 published articles: about 90 marks. 100,000 words in about
   0.25 s. No lookbehind in the patterns, so older Safari loads the page.
   The serial comma was tried and left out (most of its marks on published
   articles were not lists).
5. Performance, a filter by kind, inclusive language.
   - Performance (click the word count in the footer, or Studio):
     characters, words, sentences, paragraphs, reading and speaking time
     (250 and 130 words a minute), word and sentence length beside the
     medians of 40 open-access articles and 50 native student essays,
     Flesch reading ease and the Flesch-Kincaid grade in plain words, the
     share of different and rare words, the writing mark; saved as a text
     file.
   - The legend above the suggestions filters the list a kind at a time,
     with All to go back (INK_CATF).
   - Inclusive language (the INCL pack, style notes, never in a quotation,
     not in creative writing), each with a fix or alternatives, its reason
     and a lesson.
6. The look.
   - The saved time and the fold button sit in the footer's right corner
     at every width (the saved time carries the push; the spacer that did
     it, #stModel, is hidden).
   - By night the chosen filter chip has the same face and colours as its
     neighbours (white on light brass was hard to read); the chips keep to
     one line each.
   - Every control lifts with a deeper shadow on hover and sinks when
     pressed. The bevel rule (button:not(.sn-iconbtn):not(.sn-tool))
     outweighed the hover shadows and the pressed state of most buttons,
     so they slid 2px and did not press. One rule of the same weight now
     follows it (--popHov, --popPress); the physical primary buttons, the
     seals, the tour, the menus and the door keep their own. The engine
     pill, the Suggestions and Instruments tabs, document cards, outline
     rows and suggestion rows lift and sink too; Correct and Draft rise
     3px, brighten and press deeper. A touch listener lets Safari on
     iPhone and iPad show the pressed state (WebKit shows :active only
     when the page listens for touches). Checked with a forced-state probe
     on every control of the desk, in day and night: all lift and press
     but the two seals, which move by their own script.

MEASURED (no model, against 7.48)
---------------------------------
Write & Improve tune F0.5 0.2415 to 0.262; report 0.238 to 0.2505;
CoNLL-2014 0.2625, unchanged. Correct Wikipedia sentences changed: 64 to
72 of 1,500 (report set). After the sentence rules alone it was 73, and
eight of those nine new ones were rows that begin mid-sentence.

TRIED AND LEFT OUT
------------------
- Prepositions, articles and commas chosen by word counts from published
  English (trigrams, after Bryant and Briscoe 2018): precision 0.2 to 0.5
  on the tuning set, too low to show a writer. Pruning the counts to a
  size a page could carry lost what precision there was. The full counts
  would be about 180 MB; a pack served by the laptop link is possible
  later.
- The serial comma (see 4).

NOT DONE YET
------------
- Language variants beyond US and British (Canadian, Australian, Indian).
- Goals asked for when a new document is opened (the register and Form
  serve for now).
- A plagiarism check needs the web; detecting text written by a model has
  no honest offline method. Neither is offered.
- From 7.48: corrections inside a note are not underlined at the page
  foot; a long note is not continued on the next page; Tabula's find does
  not look inside the notes at the page foot.

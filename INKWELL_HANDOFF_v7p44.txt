INKWELL v7.44: HANDOFF NOTES (in progress)
==========================================
Date: 18 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p43.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.44.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's
signing key. The measurement data (Write & Improve, LOCNESS, CoNLL-2014,
JFLEG, the published articles, the thesis sample, the 100,000-word test
thesis) is never in the repository and never trained on.

WHAT IS IN 7.44 SO FAR
----------------------
1. BIG2, a thesis of 100,000 words (committed ed6c727). Measured in
   Chromium: opening 1.9 s, 324 pages; a keystroke 38 to 49 ms (about 40 s
   extrapolated before); a pause re-checks only the changed block (0.19 s);
   versions in IndexedDB; a reload is exact.
2. FIND1, Tabula's search, as Word's Navigation pane (src/lib/find_core.js,
   INKFIND; tools/find/find_test.js, 23). Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac) or Home,
   Find. Every result lit in the text (the CSS Custom Highlight API; past
   2,000 results, the 2,000 around the current one) and listed with its page
   and the words around it, a hundred at a time; Enter and Shift+Enter, the
   arrows, F3 and Cmd+G go from the cursor; "Back to where I was"; recent
   searches; Match case, Whole words only, Ignore accents; a straight
   apostrophe finds a curly one. Footnotes and endnotes are searched; note
   numbers and tracked deletions are not. Replace (the browser's own undo)
   and Replace all (one step back with Ctrl+Z or Undo), tracked when
   tracking is on. A Headings tab holds the outline with pages and "you are
   here" (View, Navigation). The counter bar names the page in view.
   Measured on 100,000 words: "the", 7,513 results in 114 to 141 ms;
   a phrase in about 11 ms; scrolling with them lit 18 ms a frame against
   17 ms with none; typing with the pane open about 20 ms a key.
   Also: a footnote's number was the dark ribbon colour on the night page.
3. My way of working (src/lib/myway_core.js, INKWAY; tools/myway/
   myway_test.js, 21), in Inkwell (Studio, My way of working), Tabula (View,
   Comfort), Scriba and Fontes (a button in each). Eleven supports, each off
   until switched on, one choice serving all four:
   - Start small, One next step, Draft now fix later, Today so far,
     Three at a time, Messages wait for me: on the desk.
   - Focus ruler: desk, Tabula, Scriba (the rooms dim by a style rule, so
     the document is never touched).
   - Gentle time and Quiet senses: everywhere (the band and the questions
     are drawn by the desk over the rooms; the rooms tell it of work).
   - Where was I?: all four (Fontes keeps the source being added).
   - Reading comfort: all four; Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute,
     SIL OFL 1.1, src/lib/atkinson_font_OFL.txt), held once in Inkwell's page
     and read by the rooms. Only the screen changes.
   The look is the desk's own: raised, bevelled controls that lift and
   press in, the brass-tipped switch, cards that pop out; still for Minimal
   motion, Quiet senses and reduced motion. Contrast measured on 324 texts
   in each mode: none under 4.5 to 1.
4. The build lets a room take shared scripts (@@LIB_NAME@@ in a room).
   Clepsydra gains inkClep.state for Gentle time (marked in its header).

AFTER THE 7.44 RELEASE (for 7.45)
---------------------------------
5. GAP1, Tabula's pages (committed f92c416): a paragraph runs on to the
   next page as in Word, so a long paragraph no longer leaves a blank gap at
   the foot of a page. A style rule shapes each paragraph's lines around the
   page break; the document is never touched. 100,000 words: 272 pages (306
   before), laid out in about 0.3 s; page breaks and section breaks kept.
6. G3c, the checks a doctoral editor makes (src/lib/dphil_core.js,
   INKDPHIL; tools/dphil/dphil_test.js, 94). They read the whole document
   once, short or long, and add notes where no other note stands:
   - -ise beside -ize (both British; -ize is Oxford spelling), names spared;
   - an abbreviation used before it is spelled out, spelled out and never
     used again, or spelled out twice in one chapter;
   - a citation with no entry in the References, a year that disagrees, an
     entry of a References list cited nowhere (not a Bibliography), the
     list out of alphabetical order;
   - the citations' small marks one way: "(Smith, 2019)" or "(Smith 2019)",
     "p. 4", ": 4" or ", 4", "&" or "and";
   - a quotation of a sentence or more with no page;
   - quotation marks and apostrophes, per cent, ellipses, dashes, "e.g.,":
     each written one way;
   - headings, figures and tables numbered in order; "see Figure 5" past
     the last figure; "see Chapter 7" in a thesis of six chapters;
     "Figure 2" beside "figure 2".
   Casual writing gets none; creative writing only the marks. Measured on
   the 40 published articles, the thesis sample and the 100,000-word thesis
   (read note by note; false alarms found and removed: "expertise",
   official names such as the World Health Organization, quoted titles
   before a year, the chapters of a book under discussion, titles and TV
   series before a year). About 30 ms for the 100,000 words.
   Also: the British pack no longer marks Oxford -ize as American ("analyze"
   still is); names in the document's own References are known to the
   spelling check ("Toombs" was offered "Tombs"). Shown, not yet committed:
   a note that changes nothing no longer strikes its words out, and these
   notes carry their own tag (citations, abbreviations, numbering,
   spelling, consistency).
7. CITE1, first part: Chicago notes at thesis length. Measured on the
   100,000-word thesis with 998 notes (Chicago full notes, short notes,
   Ibid., a Bibliography):
   - placing a footnote took 3.5 s, now 0.3 s; renumbering 3.2 s, now
     0.7 s. The page layout keeps every page whose text is unchanged,
     wherever it now begins (until now every page between the marker and
     its note was measured again), and a page whose text changed only in
     its figures is measured at its old fit; the pages are the same as a
     fresh layout after each kind of edit (368 pages compared).
   - a long document is checked a paragraph at a time with its markers
     taken out, and each note is a paragraph of its own, so a new number
     sends nothing to be checked again; a correction never takes a marker
     with it.
   - a marker followed by a colon in the text ("three reasons[^4]: first")
     was read as a note's definition and kept its old number on renumbering;
     fixed.
   - no false notes left in the Notes and the Bibliography (there were
     three: "2nd rev. ed." read as a sentence end, the Notes counted as one
     paragraph of 7,004 words, and 118 author names in notes marked as
     misspelt once each note was read alone).
   - the offline rewrites keep every marker (64 versions of four passages
     with markers); the model's rewrites already pass the fact guard, which
     counts markers.
   Not yet: writing a note beside its marker (now the cursor goes to the
   Notes at the end), which is a design to show first. The first check of
   a whole thesis, in the background, takes about 40 s here.

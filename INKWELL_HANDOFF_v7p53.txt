WHY 7.53
--------
The writer's standing request: the offline corrector and editor pushed
further, at a doctoral standard, teaching while it helps, with no model
needed. After 7.52 the audit by type still named missing commas and tense
as the largest silences; this release takes what was deterministic in
each.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Stage G8 (src/lib/learner_core.js; tools/grammar/README.md, "the
   opening's comma before any subject"): the comma after a fronted time
   phrase before a name or a noun subject ("One day Penny discovered",
   "The next day the people of the town went"); the opening phrase or
   clause of 7.50 with three bars lifted (a comma later in the sentence,
   a time opener read as a preposition, a clause inside the main one) and
   the guards those bars had hidden (a verb before the subject, a name and
   its verb inside the phrase, a title's capital, a regnal numeral, the
   clause after a later comma); the comma after "and" before an aside a
   comma already closes ("and, according to them,"); the verb joined by
   "and" keeping the past of the finite verb before it ("went with Ana and
   give her" → gave; "were poor and don't have" → didn't), with the
   participle, the infinitive, the present on the way and the verbs of
   argument left alone. The page gives tense its own family in Your
   patterns ("The tense, kept all the way through") with its lesson, and
   folds the old counts into it once (inkFoldStats, key 753).

   Whole page, ERRANT F0.5, from 7.52: W&I tuning half 0.311 to 0.318,
   report half 0.274 to 0.280, CoNLL-2014 0.279 to 0.284; correct prose
   changed 4.1% to 4.3% (tuning) and 4.4% to 4.5% (report). Published prose, read with
   the same harness on both pages: 388 notes (269 with a fix) to 395 notes (276 with a fix);
   the thesis sample carries none either way.

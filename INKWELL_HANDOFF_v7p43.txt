INKWELL v7.43: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 18 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p42.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.43.

Look for "v7.43" beside the date at the top. If it is not there, the device
is not running 7.43.

WHAT CAME IN 7.43
-----------------
1. W5, the web app's writing model loads in every browser. A browser with
   half precision (Chrome, Edge, Safari) was given the q4f16 files, which
   overflow in ONNX Runtime's WebGPU path; only Firefox, given q4, loaded.
   Every browser now loads q4; the model must answer once ("Reply with one
   word: ready") before it is called ready; q4f16 files left in the
   browser's storage are removed (inkWriterDropF16).
2. SC1, the writing mark (src/lib/mark_core.js, INKMARK; mark_test.js,
   26). A mark for the writing alone on the UK scale, read onto UK
   undergraduate and postgraduate classes and US letters with grade
   points from AACRAO EDGE's UK table (as reproduced by Arcadia
   University; AACRAO's own pages are for subscribers). "Writing and
   argument" halves it with the model's rubric marks. "How this mark is
   made" explains the parts and the calibration.
3. G3a, spelling to a scholarly standard (spell_core.js; spell_test.js,
   94): SCOWL size 80 (main lists, no word one slip from a common one),
   scholarly formations, names at a sentence's start, the writer's own
   terms. On 40 published articles: 7.45 flags per 1000 words to 1.58;
   gold spelling errors still found 93.0% (94.1%).
4. G3b, notes that made scholarly writing worse removed: on the articles,
   32.7 notes per 1000 words to 6.6, with learners' errors still found
   (215 true fixes against 212 on the held-out W&I half).
5. The side buttons in Tabula, Scriba, Fontes and their floating windows
   draw no shadow on the glyphs (Safari drew a colour emoji's text shadow
   as a dark square).
6. T1, Tabula's pages. The desk's text reached Tabula with each single
   line break as <br> inside one paragraph, so a document written line by
   line was one block and the pager, which breaks between blocks, showed
   one sheet. Each line is now a paragraph marked data-ink-l (a single
   break on the way back), and headings keep their level both ways
   (tabularHTMLFromText, tabularTextFromHTML). Measured: Page 1 of 1 (2
   blocks) to Page 1 of 6 (31 blocks); the round trip is exact.
7. RW1a, offline rewrites recast sentences (rewrite_core.js RECAST and
   PRECISE; tools/rewrite/cases.js, 322 cases, 92 new with their traps):
   empty openers, "there are ... who", "it is something that", "needs to
   be looked at in more detail", "the ways in which", reduced clauses,
   about thirty hidden verbs, the precise word by its noun ("a significant
   role"; "a big fan" stays), and alternatives (INKRW.alternatives). An
   audit on about 41,000 sentences fixed older faults: "a lot of different
   music" had become "much different music", "a big fan" "a large fan",
   "not totally conclusive" "not conclusive", and the reordered version
   dropped a heading.
8. RW1b, the Rewrite card change by change (src/lib/explain_core.js,
   INKEXPLAIN; tools/rewrite/explain_test.js, 45). A version is read as
   changes (GECFILTER's diff, grouped by the rule that made them, or for a
   model by sentence, merged sentences together), each with its reason,
   alternatives, and a tick to keep it or the writer's own words; "Use
   this" uses the version as chosen. "Try first" gives hints (the kind of
   change, the sentence, the first word) and checks the writer's version.
9. RW1c, model rewrites (inkWrite, inkModelVersions, INKEXPLAIN.clean and
   judge). Measured through Inkwell's own server here: LFM2.5 1.2B (the
   web app's model) dropped a citation or number in 9 of 18 rewrites and
   sometimes answered the passage; Qwen3 4B (the Mac mini's) lost none
   but once wrote Chinese characters. Now: the passage is sent labelled,
   with rules for citations, numbers and the point of view ("third
   person" is gone from every scholarly instruction, here and in both
   servers); the answer is cleaned and a dropped citation goes back into
   the sentence that carries its claim; two versions (fuller, lighter);
   an answer that describes the passage is not shown; a version that
   changes who speaks or strays far is marked; a small model says so.
   Prompt layouts tried on the small model (instruction in the system
   message, with the passage, after the passage) all stayed rough: the
   honest route to good model rewrites is the Mac mini's model.
10. DK1, night mode legible. Controls filled with the champagne accent
   kept white text (1.9 to 1); they carry dark ink now (10 to 1). The blue
   faces are a shade deeper and the Draft text lighter. Measured in the
   browser: every visible button in the desk, the rewrite card, nine
   dialogs and the three rooms, at rest, under the pointer and with each
   room tab on: none under 4.5 to 1. On a phone the round notes button
   waits while the Rewrite card is open.

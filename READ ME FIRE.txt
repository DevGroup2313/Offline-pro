INKWELL 7.29 ON THE FIRE TABLET
===============================
Three ways, best first:

1. The Fire app (recommended): build it once on GitHub and install it; see
   "READ ME ANDROID AND FIRE.txt" in Inkwell_Android_v7p29.zip. It asks for
   the microphone, saves with Android's own dialog, and keeps your library.

2. The web app: publish it as described in "READ ME iPad and iPhone.txt"
   (Inkwell_Web_v7p29.zip), open the address in Silk, and add it to the Home
   screen from Silk's menu.

3. The local file: copy Inkwell.html to the tablet and open it in Silk.
   Everything except the microphone works this way.

Each way keeps its own storage. Before switching, use File, Export library
in the old one and File, Import library in the new one.

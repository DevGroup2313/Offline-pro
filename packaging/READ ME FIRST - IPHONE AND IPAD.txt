INKWELL 7.52 FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
================================

TWO WAYS TO HAVE INKWELL ON AN IPHONE OR IPAD
- TODAY: the web app, added to the Home Screen. Nothing to install; it opens
  like an app and works offline after the first visit.
- LATER: the iPhone and iPad app (.ipa). GitHub builds it on one of its
  Macs, and GitHub Actions has not started since 10 October, so no 7.45 .ipa
  can be made yet. Its steps follow below, for when it works again. The app
  can use the Mac mini's model; the web app cannot (Safari does not let a
  secure web page call a computer on the home network).


TODAY: THE WEB APP ON THE HOME SCREEN
-------------------------------------
1. Put the web app online once (the Web App package: unzip it and drag its
   folder into Netlify; its READ ME says how). Keep the address Netlify
   gives, ending in .netlify.app.
2. On the iPhone or iPad, open that address in Safari.
3. Share, "Add to Home Screen", Add.
4. Open Inkwell from the Home Screen once while online, so it keeps its
   files for offline use. Look for "v7.52" beside the date.
- Updating later: when the site is updated on Netlify, open Inkwell from the
  Home Screen while online; the new version shows at the first or second
  opening.
- Your documents live in the Home Screen app's own storage on this device.
  Inkwell keeps earlier copies there too (File, Earlier copies). Safari can
  clear a web app's storage when the device is very short of space, so a
  copy on another device is wise for long work: File, Move the library to
  another device.


NEW IN 7.52
-----------
- The spelling fix, measured: a common word now stands clear of a rare one
  ("through", not "throng"), a long word two slips from its only common
  candidate gets its fix ("lenguaje" to "language"), three slips in a row
  are no longer taken for a phrase of another language, and every
  spelling card teaches the word itself ("Necessary: one c, two s, as a
  shirt has one collar and two sleeves").
- More fixes that need no model: the comma after "In my opinion" whatever
  follows, before an instruction ("If you have time, read it") and before
  "but" with a noun phrase; the space after a sentence's stop; the
  paragraph's missing full stop; "into" after a verb of motion; a role
  noun's article ("when I was a student"); "congratulations on".
- Lessons from the sentence itself: "Results means more than one, so the
  verb is were", on the card, in the line at the foot and in practice.
  Your patterns counts fixes under nine families, each with its lesson.
- "My friends likes" takes the plain verb; a correctness note takes the
  place of a style note on the same words ("cannot", "a lot of").
- Forty published articles read again: abstract nouns, titles, identity
  capitals, the noun "will", "(i)", plural objects and transcript codes
  no longer draw wrong fixes. A term you use twice is yours.
- The guided tour shows the teaching; Undo rides the message at the foot.
  See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p52.txt.

NEW IN 7.51
-----------
- The corrector adds the comma after an opening phrase or clause ("In the
  same way, I had", "When I arrived, my uncle was", "After some years,
  the girl decided"), measured as before: more errors found on learner
  essays, precision kept.
- Fewer wrong notes on published prose: a team or a government with a
  plain verb is noted without a fix (British usage allows it); a day or a
  year before a name is a title or an event; "six home runs", "this ends
  up", "as they pleased" and "on the night" are left alone.
- The rule after a rewrite: when you use a rewrite, one line at the foot
  of the page says the first change kept and the reason behind it, as it

- The message at the foot of the page wears the desk's own face: cream
  with a brass edge by day, midnight by night, no longer ink-black. The
  practice card's Done and Check buttons sit at their own width.
  See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p51.txt.

NEW IN 7.50
-----------
- The corrector catches more, offline and with no model: a preposition
  missing its last word ("in spite all") or the wrong one ("interested
  on"), a missing article ("is experienced person"), a singular after a
  quantity ("many problem"), the verb's form after will, can, does, have

  start; My way of working switches them off.
- Tabula saves a new document to the device (Word, PDF, text, web page)
  and prints, inside Inkwell, on every device. Inkwell is offered to open
  Word, PDF and text files from the device (Share, Open with, a double
  click), into Tabula.
- Safari: the writing model installs and runs (two faults mended).
- The seal icon fills its canvas on the Mac and on the iPhone and iPad.
- The door offers the guided tour as a quiet link above its buttons; the
  Welcome page's first example gets a proper rewrite; Tabula's side
  buttons are sized to their row.
- Ownership made explicit (LICENCE.txt: no licence is granted, permission
  is personal and revocable, the author alone distributes). The app
  checks its own signature and its own text: Studio, The Inkwell Guide,

NEW IN 7.48

NEW IN 7.45
-----------
- The checks a doctoral editor makes, on the whole document, short or
  long: -ise beside -ize (both are British; -ize is Oxford spelling); an
  abbreviation used before it is spelled out, spelled out and never used
  again, or spelled out twice; a citation with no entry in the References,
  a year that disagrees, an entry nobody cites, and the list out of
  alphabetical order; the small marks of citations kept one way; a
  quotation with no page; quotation marks, apostrophes, per cent,
  ellipses, dashes and "e.g.," each written one way; headings, figures,

  is no Figure 5.
- Chicago notes at thesis length: placing a footnote takes about a third

  A note marker followed by a colon now renumbers too. No correction ever
  takes a note marker with it.
- Tabula: a long paragraph runs on to the next page, as in Word, so no
  blank gap is left at the foot of a page.
- British English no longer marks Oxford spelling ("organize") as
  American. The names in your References are known to the spelling check.
- In Safari: the tab's seal follows dark mode, and where the browser has no
  WebGPU the writing model can now run on the processor (slowly; the

THE IPHONE AND IPAD APP (.IPA), FOR WHEN GITHUB ACTIONS WORKS AGAIN
=================================================================
THIS PACKAGE IS FOR: iPhone and iPad (one app for both).
THE FILE IT MAKES:   Inkwell-7.41-ios-buildN.ipa
NOT FOR: the Fire tablet (use the Fire tablet package and its .apk).

IMPORTANT, PLEASE READ FIRST
- An iPhone or iPad can NOT install an app by tapping a download in Safari.
  Tapping the .ipa (or an .apk) on the device only shows a white page with the
  file name. Apple requires every app to be signed with an Apple ID, so the
  .ipa is installed from a COMPUTER over the USB cable, with Sideloadly.
- GitHub builds the app on one of its own Macs, which has Xcode, so your Mac
  does not need Xcode.
- With a FREE Apple ID, Apple stops the app from opening 7 days after it was
  installed; you then reinstall it the same way (Part D). A paid Apple
  Developer account (approximately 99 US dollars per year; check Apple's
  current price) makes each install last a year.
- These steps were written without access to an iPhone, an iPad or
  Sideloadly. The build itself has run successfully on GitHub; the install
  steps follow Sideloadly's published method. Check Sideloadly's own current
  instructions if a screen does not match.


PART A. PUT THE PROJECT ON GITHUB (ONCE)
----------------------------------------
1. Sign in at github.com. Top right: "+", then "New repository".
   Name it, for example, inkwell-ios. Private is fine. Leave every other
   option as it is and press "Create repository".

2. Unzip this package on your computer. Open the folder inside it.

3. Make hidden files visible, so the folder ".github" can be seen:
     Windows: File Explorer, View, Show, Hidden items.
     Mac: in Finder press Cmd + Shift + full stop.

4. On the new repository's page, click "uploading an existing file".
   Select EVERYTHING inside the package folder (the folders .github, Sources,
   Resources and web, and every file beside them) and drag it onto the page.
   Drag the CONTENTS, not the package folder itself.
   Wait until every file is listed (the largest file is about 21 MB), then
   press "Commit changes".

5. Check the repository's main page. It must show:
     .github   Resources   Sources   web   project.yml
   If ".github" is missing, the uploader skipped it. Then:
     Add file, Create new file. As the name, type exactly:
        .github/workflows/build-ios.yml
     Open "WORKFLOW build-ios.yml (copy).txt" from this package, copy ALL of
     its text, paste it into the big box, and press "Commit changes".


PART B. BUILD THE .IPA
----------------------
6. Open the "Actions" tab. A run called "Build Inkwell for iPhone and iPad"
   starts by itself after each commit. If none is running: click its name on
   the left, "Run workflow", then the green "Run workflow" button.
   If GitHub asks you to enable Actions, enable them.
   Note: GitHub's Macs use your free Actions minutes about ten times faster
   than its Linux machines on a private repository (approximately; check
   GitHub's current billing page). One build takes only a few minutes.

7. Wait for the green tick.

8. On the main page, open "Releases" (right-hand side). The newest release,
   "Inkwell 7.41 for iPhone and iPad (build N)", has the .ipa under "Assets".


PART C. INSTALL BY USB (FROM A WINDOWS COMPUTER)
------------------------------------------------
Once, on the computer:
 9. Install Sideloadly from its official site, sideloadly.io.
10. Install Apple's iTunes and iCloud for Windows. Sideloadly's site says
    which versions it needs (at the time of writing, the ones downloaded from
    Apple's own website, not the Microsoft Store).

Each install:
11. Download the .ipa from the release (step 8) onto the computer.
12. Connect the iPhone or iPad with its USB cable. Unlock it, and tap "Trust"
    if it asks whether to trust this computer.
13. Open Sideloadly. Drag the .ipa into its window. Choose the device in the
    list, type your Apple ID (email), and press Start. Enter your Apple ID
    password, and the code Apple sends, when Sideloadly asks for them.

On the device, the first time only:
14. Settings, General, VPN & Device Management: tap your Apple ID, then
    "Trust".
15. Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode: switch it on, and let the
    device restart (iOS 16 and later ask for this).

16. Inkwell is now on the Home Screen. iPhone and iPad always show app icons
    as rounded squares, so the seal sits on a navy square.


PART D. EVERY 7 DAYS (FREE APPLE ID)
------------------------------------
Repeat steps 11 to 13 with the same .ipa, or a newer one, before 7 days have
passed. Installing over the app keeps your documents, because its storage
belongs to its identifier (studio.inkwell.app), and Inkwell also keeps its
own earlier copies (File, Earlier copies). If the app has already stopped
opening, reinstalling it the same way brings it back.


PART E. YOUR DOCUMENTS
----------------------
- The app keeps its own library, separate from Safari and the web app.
  To bring documents in: in the old place, File, Move the library to
  another device, Save the library as a file; in the app, the same menu,
  Open a library file from another device.
- Saving a file opens Apple's "Save to Files" dialog.
- Deleting the app deletes its storage, its earlier copies included: move
  the library first (as above) if you ever delete it.
- Scriba: the first time it records, iOS asks for the microphone; choose
  Allow. On-device Whisper downloads its model the first time, so be online
  for the first recording.


PART E2. CORRECTIONS AND MODELS ON THIS DEVICE (7.34)
-----------------------------------------------------
- New in 7.34: on an iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence
  switched on, Inkwell uses Apple's own on-device language model. It runs
  on the device, offline, and is far stronger than any model an app can
  carry. Engine shows "Apple's on-device model: available" when it is there,
  or the reason it is not (older iOS, a device without Apple Intelligence,
  or Apple Intelligence switched off in Settings).
  Not verified: this could not be tested on a real iPhone or iPad here. The
  build compiles the code against Apple's framework; the first real test is
  yours.
- Every correction from Apple's model passes Inkwell's safety filter (no
  changes to names, numbers, quotations or citations), and every rewrite is
  checked: one that drops a citation or number, or adds a name, is marked in
  red with exactly what it changed.
- Without Apple's model, corrections come from the rules engine, offline;
  it shows only correctness unless you press "Show clarity and style notes".
- No other model runs inside the app on a phone or tablet: the useful ones
  need more memory than iOS gives an app, and iOS closes the app.
- Cite (Insert, Citation): the same engine and styles Zotero uses (APA,
  Harvard, Chicago, OSCOLA, MHRA, MLA, IEEE, Nature), offline. Adding a
  source by DOI or ISBN needs the internet; BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON do not.

NEW IN 7.36: SPELLING, AND A CAREFUL GRAMMAR MODEL (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- A dictionary spelling checker now runs on this device, offline, with
  suggestions and "Add to dictionary" (switch: Studio, Rules).
- Engine, "Turn on the grammar model" (about 124 MB, once): it reads each
  paragraph as you pause and adds suggestions; nothing changes unless you
  accept. Only its reliable kinds are shown (agreement, articles,
  prepositions, spelling, verb forms). Measured: 80% to 86% of its
  suggestions match a human corrector's; on a 5,486-word thesis it
  suggested nothing.
- Coach menu: an authorship report, and "Grade against a rubric".


NEW IN 7.36: SCRIBA, LIKE CAPTION.ED, OFFLINE
--------------------------------------------
Scriba (the microphone room) now works on a recording the way Caption.Ed
does. Open a recording (or "Import audio or video") and press Transcribe:
- Playback and editing: the transcript follows the sound; a time plays from
  there; every paragraph can be corrected in place; search; exports as
  text, Word, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or straight into Inkwell's document.
- Notes and highlights: Important, Question, Action, Revisit; a note on
  any moment; a scratchpad; slides from a PDF or photos of the board.
- Your words: names and terms spelled your way in every transcript, and a
  switch that removes "um", "uh" and "er".
- Speakers: name them by hand, or press "Find the speakers". This runs on
  the device with two small open models (about 33 MB, fetched once with
  the internet, then offline). Measured on recorded meetings: about 85% of
  speech lands in a paragraph naming the right person, about 95% when the
  speakers are found before transcribing (Scriba then starts a new
  paragraph where the speaker changes). The number of speakers is often
  wrong on short recordings; check the names, and give two speakers the
  same name to join them. Details: tools/speakers/README.md.
- Summarise: a summary with chapters, action items, meeting minutes,
  revision notes with questions, or your own prompt. These come from the
  writing model Inkwell is running on your devices; none is sent anywhere
  else. Any name or number the transcript does not contain is marked so
  you can check it. With a small model the summaries are short and
  plain, and they repeat the speech model's mishearings.


YOUR COMPUTER'S MODEL
---------------------
- Stronger rewriting and drafting come from a computer over the same
  Wi-Fi: the Mac mini, or a laptop. On the computer (Inkwell for Mac or
  Windows): Engine, "Share with my other devices on this Wi-Fi"; it shows
  an address and a 6-digit code. Inkwell must stay open there. On this
  device: Engine, "Your computer's model": enter both, then "Connect to my
  computer".


PART F. UPDATING INKWELL LATER
------------------------------
- New Inkwell page: upload the new Inkwell.html into web/ (replace the old
  one); new vendor files go into web/vendor/.
- New version number: in project.yml, change MARKETING_VERSION: "7.41".
- New icon: replace Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png
  with a 1024 x 1024 PNG with NO transparency (iOS fills transparent parts
  with black). "artwork/inkwell-seal-1024.png" is the seal to make it from.
- Every commit starts a new build; install its .ipa as in Part C.


PROBLEMS
--------
- No run appears in Actions: the workflow file is missing. Do step 5 again.
- "No project.yml in this repository" or "No Inkwell.html with a vendor
  folder beside it": a folder was not uploaded. Compare the repository with
  the package and upload what is missing.
- A red step called "Publish a release": in the repository, Settings, Actions,
  General, Workflow permissions: choose "Read and write permissions", Save,
  and run the workflow again.
- A red "Build the app" step: the full log is attached to the run as
  "ios-build-log".
- Sideloadly reports a limit: a free Apple ID can have only a small number of
  sideloaded apps on a device at once (3, as far as I know), and a limited
  number of new app identifiers per week.

INKWELL 7.36 FOR THE MAC MINI (APPLE SILICON, 16 GB)
====================================================
THIS PACKAGE IS FOR: the Mac mini, and any Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and
later). For an Intel Mac (the 2019 MacBook Pro) use the Mac package and its
Intel disk image.

WHAT IS INSIDE
--------------
- Inkwell-7.36-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg: the ready-made app. No Python, no pip,
  no command line tools. (If the .dmg is not in this folder, download it
  from the GitHub release "Inkwell 7.36 for Mac (build N)", under Assets.)
- Everything GitHub needs to build it again (see the Mac READ ME).

WHY THE OLD INSTALLER FAILED ON THIS MAC
---------------------------------------
"Inkwell has opened in your browser, because the component that draws its
own window is not installed": the old installer built Inkwell from Python
on the Mac. A new Mac offers Apple's own Python 3.9 with an old pip, which
can no longer install the current window component (pywebview and PyObjC),
so the terminal asked for updates the instructions never fetched. The app
in the .dmg carries its own Python and window component inside it, so
none of that is needed any more. You do not need to install or update
Python for Inkwell.


PART A. INSTALL
---------------
1. If an older Inkwell opens: File, Export library. Keep the file.
2. Open Inkwell-7.36-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg. Drag Inkwell onto Applications.
   If asked, Replace.
3. Open Inkwell from Applications. The first time, macOS refuses ("Apple
   could not verify..."): the app is not signed with a paid Apple
   certificate. System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, "Open
   Anyway" beside Inkwell, confirm. Only once.
4. Look for "v7.36" beside the date at the top. If your documents are not
   there: File, Import library, and choose the file from step 1.


PART B. THE WRITING MODEL ON THE MAC MINI
-----------------------------------------
Engine, "Writing model on this computer": the panel marks the model
recommended for this Mac's memory. Press Install. It downloads once (about
2.5 GB of your 256 GB), then runs on the Mac mini's graphics cores (Metal),
offline. Correct, Rewrite, Draft, Rewrite with a prompt and the prompt
library use it; so do your tablets over the Wi-Fi (Part C).

Which model, and why not a bigger one: on 6 October 2026 I measured the
candidates that fit in 16 GB, including Ministral 3 (3B and 14B), Qwen3.5
(4B and 9B), LFM2.5 and Llama 3.2. The best careful editor was Qwen3 4B
Instruct 2507: behind Inkwell's safety filter it improved 40% of learner
sentences, made 9% worse, changed only 8% of sentences that were already
correct, and kept every citation in every rewrite. The larger models
changed far more correct text (Qwen3.5 9B a third, Ministral 3 14B half)
and dropped citations, which is the "rewrites my paragraph and adds things"
problem you reported. So the Mac mini runs the same measured model as the
laptops; what it adds is speed and room for long documents. Its speed on
the Mac mini could not be measured here (no Apple hardware in this
sandbox).

Every rewrite, from any engine, is checked: one that drops a citation or a
number, or adds a name or number your text does not contain, is marked in
red with exactly what changed, and its button reads "Use anyway".


PART C. YOUR TABLETS USE THE MAC MINI'S MODEL
---------------------------------------------
1. On the Mac mini: Engine, "Share with my tablets on this Wi-Fi". It shows
   an address and a 6-digit code. If macOS asks to allow Inkwell on the
   local network, choose Allow.
2. On the Fire tablet or iPad, on the same Wi-Fi: Engine, "Your laptop's
   model": enter the address and the code, then "Connect to my laptop".
Only the writing tools are shared, and only with the code. Your documents
are never reachable from the network. "Stop sharing" closes it again.


NEW IN 7.36: SCRIBA, LIKE CAPTION.ED, OFFLINE
--------------------------------------------
Scriba (the microphone room) now works on a recording the way Caption.Ed
does. Open a recording (or "Import audio or video") and press Transcribe:
- Playback and editing: the transcript follows the sound; a time plays from
  there; every paragraph can be corrected in place; search; exports as
  text, Word, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or straight into Inkwell's document.
- Notes and highlights: Important, Question, Action, Revisit; a note on
  any moment; a scratchpad; slides from a PDF or photos of the board.
- Your words: names and terms spelled your way in every transcript, and a
  switch that removes "um", "uh" and "er".
- Speakers: name them by hand, or press "Find the speakers". This runs on
  the device with two small open models (about 33 MB, fetched once with
  the internet, then offline). Measured on recorded meetings: about 85% of
  speech lands in a paragraph naming the right person, about 95% when the
  speakers are found before transcribing (Scriba then starts a new
  paragraph where the speaker changes). The number of speakers is often
  wrong on short recordings; check the names, and give two speakers the
  same name to join them. Details: tools/speakers/README.md.
- Summarise: a summary with chapters, action items, meeting minutes,
  revision notes with questions, or your own prompt. These come from the
  writing model Inkwell is running on your devices; none is sent anywhere
  else. Any name or number the transcript does not contain is marked so
  you can check it. On the Mac mini the summaries come from the
  writing model you installed in Part B; they repeat the speech model's
  mishearings.


PART D. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-------------------------------
- The writing model will not start: Engine shows the reason in words; the
  runtime's own log is in ~/Library/Application Support/Inkwell/
  inkwell_runtime.log.
- Scriba: the first time you press Record, allow the microphone.
- Before reinstalling anything: File, Export library.

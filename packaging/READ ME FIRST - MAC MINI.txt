INKWELL 7.51 FOR THE MAC MINI (APPLE SILICON, 16 GB)
====================================================

NEW IN 7.51
-----------
- The corrector adds the comma after an opening phrase or clause ("In the
  same way, I had", "When I arrived, my uncle was", "After some years,
  the girl decided"), measured as before: more errors found on learner
  essays, precision kept.
- Fewer wrong notes on published prose: a team or a government with a
  plain verb is noted without a fix (British usage allows it); a day or a
  year before a name is a title or an event; "six home runs", "this ends
  up", "as they pleased" and "on the night" are left alone.
- The rule after a rewrite: when you use a rewrite, one line at the foot
  of the page says the first change kept and the reason behind it, as it
  does after an accepted fix.
- The message at the foot of the page wears the desk's own face: cream
  with a brass edge by day, midnight by night, no longer ink-black. The
  practice card's Done and Check buttons sit at their own width.
  See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p51.txt.

NEW IN 7.50
-----------
- The corrector catches more, offline and with no model: a preposition
  missing its last word ("in spite all") or the wrong one ("interested
  on"), a missing article ("is experienced person"), a singular after a
  quantity ("many problem"), the verb's form after will, can, does, have
  and "to" ("will delighted", "have went", "a way to learning"),
  agreement ("Sharing rooms have", "the chances of accident is"), a
  capital inside a sentence ("thinking It would"), and slips such as
  "nowaday". Measured against 7.49 on learner essays: about a tenth more
  errors found, precision kept, correct prose touched no more than
  before. Every note carries its lesson.
- Learning as you go: when you accept a fix, one line at the foot of the
  page says the rule behind it, and goes. Once a day, one sentence of
  your own that you mended on an earlier day comes back, to mend from
  memory. Your patterns groups your slips by family. Both are on from the
  start; My way of working switches them off.
- Tabula saves a new document to the device (Word, PDF, text, web page)
  and prints, inside Inkwell, on every device. Inkwell is offered to open
  Word, PDF and text files from the device (Share, Open with, a double
  click), into Tabula.
- Safari: the writing model installs and runs (two faults mended).
- The seal icon fills its canvas on the Mac and on the iPhone and iPad.
- The door offers the guided tour as a quiet link above its buttons; the
  Welcome page's first example gets a proper rewrite; Tabula's side
  buttons are sized to their row.
- Ownership made explicit (LICENCE.txt: no licence is granted, permission
  is personal and revocable, the author alone distributes). The app
  checks its own signature and its own text: Studio, The Inkwell Guide,
  "This copy". See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p50.txt.

NEW IN 7.39
-----------
- Before you install: in the old Inkwell, File, Export library. Keep the file.
- Alternatives and Rewrite open a card right beside the words you selected
  (on a phone, at the foot of the screen), so you see at once what is
  offered. Nothing changes until you press "Use this"; then Undo shows.
- Alternatives for a word: words to use instead, by meaning, in the word's
  own form ("bigger" gives "larger"). For a sentence: the sentence said
  plainer, more formally and casually, and idioms said three ways ("He lost
  his train of thought": "He lost track of what he was saying", "He lost the
  thread of his argument", "He forgot what he was going to say").
- Rewrite (the new ↻ button, or Rewrite ▾): Scholarly, Professional, Casual,
  Plainer, Concise, Confident, Shorter and Other words, as tabs, each with up
  to three versions and every change with its reason. All of it works with
  no writing model; a model adds its own version.
- The words come from WordNet 3.0 (Princeton University; licence in
  vendor/WORDNET-LICENCE.txt) and a list of common alternatives written for
  Inkwell.

NEW IN 7.38
-----------
- Before you install: in the old Inkwell, File, Export library. Keep the file.
- Rewrite with a prompt now works with no writing model at all. Every

  when you open Inkwell from Applications.
- The Mac mini shares its model with every device now, the MacBook Pro and

THIS PACKAGE IS FOR: the Mac mini, and any Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and
later). For an Intel Mac (the 2019 MacBook Pro) use the Mac package and its
Intel disk image.

WHAT IS INSIDE
--------------
- Inkwell-7.39-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg: the ready-made app. No Python, no pip,
  no command line tools. (If the .dmg is not in this folder, download it
  from the GitHub release "Inkwell 7.39 for Mac (build N)", under Assets.)
- Everything GitHub needs to build it again (see the Mac READ ME).

WHY THE OLD INSTALLER FAILED ON THIS MAC
---------------------------------------
"Inkwell has opened in your browser, because the component that draws its
own window is not installed": the old installer built Inkwell from Python
on the Mac. A new Mac offers Apple's own Python 3.9 with an old pip, which
can no longer install the current window component (pywebview and PyObjC),
so the terminal asked for updates the instructions never fetched. The app
in the .dmg carries its own Python and window component inside it, so
none of that is needed any more. You do not need to install or update
Python for Inkwell.


PART A. INSTALL
---------------
1. If an older Inkwell opens: File, Export library. Keep the file.
2. Open Inkwell-7.39-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg. Drag Inkwell onto Applications.
   If asked, Replace.
3. Open Inkwell from Applications. The first time, macOS refuses ("Apple
   could not verify..."): the app is not signed with a paid Apple
   certificate. System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, "Open
   Anyway" beside Inkwell, confirm. Only once.
   The disk "Inkwell" that the .dmg put on your Desktop is only the
   installer; your documents are never on it. From 7.38, Inkwell puts it
   away by itself when you open Inkwell from Applications. To put it away
   by hand: right-click it on the Desktop and choose Eject.
4. Look for "v7.51" beside the date at the top. If your documents are not
   there: File, Import library, and choose the file from step 1.


PART B. THE WRITING MODEL ON THE MAC MINI
-----------------------------------------
Engine, "Writing model on this computer": the panel marks the model
recommended for this Mac's memory. Press Install. It downloads once (about
2.5 GB of your 256 GB), then runs on the Mac mini's graphics cores (Metal),
offline. Correct, Rewrite, Draft, Rewrite with a prompt and the prompt
library use it; so do your tablets over the Wi-Fi (Part C).

Which model, and why not a bigger one: on 6 October 2026 I measured the
candidates that fit in 16 GB, including Ministral 3 (3B and 14B), Qwen3.5
(4B and 9B), LFM2.5 and Llama 3.2. The best careful editor was Qwen3 4B
Instruct 2507: behind Inkwell's safety filter it improved 40% of learner
sentences, made 9% worse, changed only 8% of sentences that were already
correct, and kept every citation in every rewrite. The larger models
changed far more correct text (Qwen3.5 9B a third, Ministral 3 14B half)
and dropped citations, which is the "rewrites my paragraph and adds things"
problem you reported. So the Mac mini runs the same measured model as the
laptops; what it adds is speed and room for long documents. Its speed on
the Mac mini could not be measured here (no Apple hardware in this
sandbox).

Every rewrite, from any engine, is checked: one that drops a citation or a
number, or adds a name or number your text does not contain, is marked in
red with exactly what changed, and its button reads "Use anyway".


PART C. YOUR OTHER DEVICES USE THE MAC MINI'S MODEL
---------------------------------------------------
1. On the Mac mini: Engine, "Share with my other devices on this Wi-Fi".
   It shows an address and a 6-digit code. If macOS asks to allow Inkwell
   on the local network, choose Allow. Keep Inkwell open on the Mac mini
   (it may sit in the Dock); when it is closed, nothing is shared.
2. On the Fire tablet, iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi-Fi: Engine, "Your
   computer's model": enter the address and the code, then "Connect to my
   computer".
3. New in 7.38, on the MacBook Pro or a Windows laptop, on the same Wi-Fi:
   Engine, "Another computer's model": enter the address and the code,
   then "Use that computer's model". The pill at the top reads "Wi-Fi" and
   the Mac mini's model. When the Mac mini is asleep or closed, the
   laptop's own model stands in, and the writing goes back to the Mac mini
   when it answers again. "Back to this computer's model" undoes it.
Only the writing and voice tools are shared, and only with the code. Your
documents are never reachable from the network. "Stop sharing" closes it.
The web app in Safari cannot reach the Mac mini: a secure web page may not
call a plain address on the home network. The iPhone and iPad app can.


NEW IN 7.36: SPELLING, AND A CAREFUL GRAMMAR MODEL (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- A dictionary spelling checker now runs on this device, offline, with
  suggestions and "Add to dictionary" (switch: Studio, Rules).
- Engine, "Turn on the grammar model" (about 124 MB, once): it reads each
  paragraph as you pause and adds suggestions; nothing changes unless you
  accept. Only its reliable kinds are shown (agreement, articles,
  prepositions, spelling, verb forms). Measured: 80% to 86% of its
  suggestions match a human corrector's; on a 5,486-word thesis it
  suggested nothing.
- Coach menu: an authorship report, and "Grade against a rubric".


NEW IN 7.36: SCRIBA, LIKE CAPTION.ED, OFFLINE
--------------------------------------------
Scriba (the microphone room) now works on a recording the way Caption.Ed
does. Open a recording (or "Import audio or video") and press Transcribe:
- Playback and editing: the transcript follows the sound; a time plays from
  there; every paragraph can be corrected in place; search; exports as
  text, Word, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or straight into Inkwell's document.
- Notes and highlights: Important, Question, Action, Revisit; a note on
  any moment; a scratchpad; slides from a PDF or photos of the board.
- Your words: names and terms spelled your way in every transcript, and a
  switch that removes "um", "uh" and "er".
- Speakers: name them by hand, or press "Find the speakers". This runs on
  the device with two small open models (about 33 MB, fetched once with
  the internet, then offline). Measured on recorded meetings: about 85% of
  speech lands in a paragraph naming the right person, about 95% when the
  speakers are found before transcribing (Scriba then starts a new
  paragraph where the speaker changes). The number of speakers is often
  wrong on short recordings; check the names, and give two speakers the
  same name to join them. Details: tools/speakers/README.md.
- Summarise: a summary with chapters, action items, meeting minutes,
  revision notes with questions, or your own prompt. These come from the
  writing model Inkwell is running on your devices; none is sent anywhere
  else. Any name or number the transcript does not contain is marked so
  you can check it. On the Mac mini the summaries come from the
  writing model you installed in Part B; they repeat the speech model's
  mishearings.


PART D. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-------------------------------
- The writing model will not start: Engine shows the reason in words; the
  runtime's own log is in ~/Library/Application Support/Inkwell/
  inkwell_runtime.log.
- Scriba: the first time you press Record, allow the microphone.
- Before reinstalling anything: File, Export library.

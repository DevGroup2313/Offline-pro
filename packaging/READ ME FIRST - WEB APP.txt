INKWELL 7.45: THE WEB APP
=========================

WHAT IS IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------
Unzipping this package gives one folder, "Inkwell_Web_App_v7p45". That
folder IS the finished web site: index.html is at its top, beside sw.js, the
manifest, and the icons and vendor folders. There is nothing to build and no
GitHub Actions is needed. No file in it is larger than 9 MB.

(This READ ME and the licence sit in the folder too, so they go online with
the site. They hold nothing private.)

Safari can add Inkwell to the Home Screen only from a web address, not from
a file. So the folder is put online once, at an address of your own.

PUT IT ONLINE THE FIRST TIME (choose one)
-----------------------------------------
A. Netlify (free, and the simplest)
   1. On a computer, open  https://app.netlify.com/drop  in a browser and
      log in (free). A site dropped without an account is kept behind a
      temporary password until it is claimed.
   2. Unzip this package. Drag the folder "Inkwell_Web_App_v7p45" onto the
      page. Drag the FOLDER, not the .zip file: Netlify asks for the
      unzipped folder.
   3. Netlify shows the address, ending in .netlify.app. Keep it. Open it:
      Inkwell should appear, with "v7.45" beside the date at the top.
   Netlify's guidance is that a drop works best under 50 MB; this site is
   about 66 MB, most of it the files for the optional models. If the drop
   stalls, try once more on a steady connection, or use B.

B. GitHub Pages (free for a PUBLIC repository)
   1. github.com, "+", New repository, for example inkwell-site. Choose
      Public. Create repository.
   2. "uploading an existing file": open the folder "Inkwell_Web_App_v7p45"
      and drag in EVERYTHING INSIDE it (index.html, sw.js,
      manifest.webmanifest, and the icons and vendor folders). Commit.
   3. Settings, Pages. Source: "Deploy from a branch". Branch: main, folder
      / (root). Save. After a minute or two the address appears, of the form
      https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/inkwell-site/
   A public repository can be read by anyone; your documents are never in
   it (they stay on your devices). GitHub publishes Pages with its own
   Actions; if Actions is blocked on your account, the site may not
   publish. Then use A.


ON THE IPHONE OR IPAD
---------------------
1. Open the address in Safari.
2. Share, "Add to Home Screen", Add.
3. Open Inkwell from the Home Screen once while online, so it keeps its
   files for offline use.


UPDATING LATER (each new version)
---------------------------------
Nothing to do first: updating the site at the same address keeps your
documents.
1. Netlify: open your site in Netlify, then Deploys. Drag the new unzipped
   folder onto the drop area at the foot of the Deploys page. The address
   stays the same.
   GitHub: in your site repository, "Add file", "Upload files", drag in
   everything inside the new folder, Commit. The old files are replaced.
2. Open Inkwell once while online. The new version shows at the first
   opening (an update from 7.37 or older may need a second opening).
Look for "v7.45" beside the date at the top.


YOUR OWN ADDRESS (A DOMAIN NAME), IF YOU WANT ONE
-------------------------------------------------
The .netlify.app address always works. A domain of your own is optional,
and is set up in two places: at Netlify and at the company the domain was
bought from (for example Namecheap).

1. At Netlify: open the Inkwell site (its own site, not another project's),
   Domain management, and add the domain. Netlify then lists the records it
   wants: Domain management, Production domains, "Pending DNS verification"
   beside the domain.
2. At the domain company (Namecheap: Domain List, Manage, Advanced DNS),
   the records must point only to Netlify. Netlify's guide gives these:
     ALIAS Record  Host @     Value apex-loadbalancer.netlify.com
                   (or, where ALIAS is not offered: A Record, Host @,
                   Value 75.2.60.5)
     CNAME Record  Host www   Value YOUR-SITE.netlify.app
   Delete every other A record for @ and www. In particular, an address
   such as 192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x or 172.16.x.x to 172.31.x.x is a home
   network address: no one outside that home can reach it. With such a
   record, browsers can show "This site can't be reached" or
   DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN.
3. Netlify sets up the HTTPS certificate by itself (Let's Encrypt) once the
   records point to it; Domain management, HTTPS shows its state. Changes
   can take up to 48 hours to reach every network. Until then, use the
   .netlify.app address.
4. Check the domain is linked to the right site: open the Inkwell site in
   Netlify, Domain management. If Netlify says another site has the
   domain, remove it there first. A domain linked to no site, or to a site
   without index.html at its top, shows Netlify's "Page not found".
Netlify's guides:
  https://docs.netlify.com/manage/domains/configure-domains/configure-external-dns/
  https://docs.netlify.com/manage/domains/secure-domains-with-https/https-ssl/

One domain, one site: if a domain already shows another project (for
example a study companion), connecting it to Inkwell moves it away from that
project. Give each project its own Netlify site and its own address.


YOUR DOCUMENTS
--------------
- The web app keeps its own library in the browser, separate from the
  apps. Updating the site at the same address keeps it, and Inkwell keeps
  earlier copies there by itself (File, Earlier copies).
- A NEW address (for example, moving from the .netlify.app address to your
  own domain) starts with an empty library, because a browser keeps each
  address's storage apart. Carry it across once: at the old address, File,
  Move the library to another device, Save the library as a file; at the
  new address, the same menu, Open a library file from another device.
- A browser may clear a web app's storage when the device is very short of
  space. For long work, keep a copy on another device the same way.


NEW IN 7.47
-----------
- The Engine panel shows the writing model's install as it happens: each
  step, a bar, the megabytes so far and the time so far. Until now only the
  pill at the top showed the download, and the panel's veil hid it.
- A browser without WebGPU gets the way to turn it on, step by step (in
  Safari: Settings, Advanced, "Show features for web developers"; then
  Settings, Feature Flags, WebGPU; then reload), or Chrome or Edge. Only
  then, for a browser that cannot have WebGPU, two slower ways on the
  processor: the smaller model (about 480 MB, about 2 GB of memory) or the
  full model (about 1.8 GB, about 5 GB of memory).
- The tab's seal is chosen for night mode before the browser reads any
  icon (Safari keeps the first icon it reads).
- The corrector, measured under a doctoral examiner's eye:
  - On 40 published articles, spelling "fixes" fell from 111, nearly all
    wrong ("intubation" to "incubation"), to 13. Words built by rule from
    known ones, British spellings, Latin and other languages, names and
    speech written as spoken are left alone. Real misspellings in learner
    essays are still found (92.2%, before 93.0%).
  - Of 70 errors an examiner marks, planted in scholarly sentences, 57 are
    caught (before 30): affect and effect, complement, discrete,
    "comprises of", ensure, elicit, elude, proceed, whether, pore, loath,
    rein in, flout, their, who and whose, "between the author and I",
    "one of the factors is", "these findings suggest", "they risk",
    "however" between sentences, a comma between subject and verb, and the
    dangling opener ("Having analysed the data, the conclusion is").
  - Fewer wrong notes on correct prose: "Table 2" keeps its figure, "eg"
    used throughout is the house style (Oxford's "e.g." takes no comma),
    "the principle, that each life is equal" keeps its comma, names keep
    their spelling, and the verb is no longer matched to a noun across a
    clause.


NEW IN 7.46
-----------
- In a browser without WebGPU (Safari on some Macs), installing the
  writing model no longer brings the page down. 7.45 ran the full model
  there on the processor. Measured, the page then held about 5.4 GB of
  memory, and a browser short of memory closes the page. Now a smaller
  model runs there: LFM2.5 350M, one download of about 480 MB. Measured,
  the page holds at most about 2 GB, and a sentence takes about 20 seconds
  on Inkwell's test machine. Inkwell asks before it downloads it. Its
  rewrites are weaker than the full model's. Chrome or Edge (with WebGPU),
  or the Inkwell app for Mac, run the full model.
- If Inkwell closes while a model is loading, that model no longer loads
  again by itself the next time Inkwell opens. This guard had not worked
  since 7.34.
- The 1.77 GB of model files that 7.45 downloaded for the processor are
  removed from the browser's storage.
- A dropped download of the writing model's engine is tried again.
- The Engine panel no longer speaks of a companion server on the web
  app's site, which has none.


NEW IN 7.45
-----------
- The checks a doctoral editor makes, on the whole document, short or
  long: -ise beside -ize (both are British; -ize is Oxford spelling); an
  abbreviation used before it is spelled out, spelled out and never used
  again, or spelled out twice; a citation with no entry in the References,
  a year that disagrees, an entry nobody cites, and the list out of
  alphabetical order; the small marks of citations kept one way; a
  quotation with no page; quotation marks, apostrophes, per cent,
  ellipses, dashes and "e.g.," each written one way; headings, figures,
  tables and chapters numbered in order, and no "see Figure 5" when there
  is no Figure 5.
- Chicago notes at thesis length: placing a footnote takes about a third
  of a second (it took three and a half), and renumbering under a second.
  A note marker followed by a colon now renumbers too. No correction ever
  takes a note marker with it.
- Tabula: a long paragraph runs on to the next page, as in Word, so no
  blank gap is left at the foot of a page.
- British English no longer marks Oxford spelling ("organize") as
  American. The names in your References are known to the spelling check.
- In Safari: the tab's seal follows dark mode, and where the browser has no
  WebGPU the writing model can now run on the processor (slowly; the
  Engine panel says how slowly before you choose it).

NEW IN 7.44
-----------
- A thesis of 100,000 words opens in about two seconds, and typing stays
  quick (about 40 ms a key, where it would have taken many seconds).
  Checking runs in the background and shows how far it has got. Saved
  versions are kept in the browser's larger store, so a long document is
  safe.
- Tabula searches like Word. Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac, or Home, Find) opens
  the Navigation pane: every result is highlighted and listed with its
  page and the words around it. Enter goes to the next, Shift+Enter to the
  one before, and "Back to where I was" returns you. Replace and Replace
  all can be undone; with Track changes on, they are tracked. Footnotes
  are searched too. The Headings tab shows the outline, with the pages and
  "you are here".
- My way of working (Studio, in Inkwell; View, Comfort, in Tabula; a
  button in Scriba and Fontes): eleven supports, each off until you switch
  it on. Start small, One next step, Draft now fix later, Focus ruler,
  Gentle time, Where was I?, Today so far, Three at a time, Messages wait
  for me, Quiet senses, and Reading comfort (text size, spacing, the clear
  Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, and the tint of the paper). One choice
  serves all four apps.
- A footnote's number is legible in night mode.

NEW IN 7.43
-----------
- Night mode is legible: the selected tab and the other light-gold
  buttons now carry dark letters (white on them measured 1.9 to 1), and
  the blue buttons are a shade deeper. Every button in the desk, the
  dialogs and the three rooms measured 4.5 to 1 or better.
- Tabula shows real pages for a document written line by line (before, it
  showed one long sheet), and headings keep their level both ways.
- Rewrite recasts whole sentences offline, with no model: "It is very
  clear that this is a really important issue" becomes "This is an
  important issue"; "There are many students who struggle" becomes "Many
  students struggle"; "make use of" becomes "use"; "the ways in which"
  becomes "how"; "a big role" becomes "a significant role".
- Every change in a rewrite shows why it was made, offers other ways to
  say it ("Clearly, this is an..."), and has a tick to keep it or keep
  your own words instead. "Try first" lets you find the changes yourself
  from hints, then compare with Inkwell's version.
- A writing model now gives two versions, a fuller one and a lighter one
  closer to your words. It keeps your citations; an answer that only
  describes your passage is not shown; a version that changes who is
  speaking, or strays far from your words, says so.
- The writing mark is out of 100 on the UK scale and read onto UK and US
  grades, undergraduate and postgraduate (the US letters follow the
  AACRAO EDGE table). Choose Writing, or Writing and argument.
- Scholarly writing is no longer marked down: four fifths of the style
  notes on published scholarship were wrong and are gone, and the spelling
  checker knows scholarly vocabulary, names, and the terms you use often.
- The writing model in the browser window loads in Chrome, Edge and
  Safari, not only Firefox, and must answer once before it is called
  ready.
- The side buttons in Tabula, Scriba and Fontes, and in their floating
  windows, no longer have a heavy square shadow.

NEW IN 7.42
-----------
- Inkwell catches more mistakes offline, with no model: the errors people

- The web app's tab icon follows the theme: the night seal in dark mode.
- An iPhone or iPad with no app can open Inkwell from the Mac mini itself:

- The writing model's download (web app) keeps its progress on the pill,

NEW IN 7.40

NEW IN 7.39

OFFLINE, AND THE MODELS
-----------------------
- After the first visit the web app works with no internet: the rules
  engine, spelling, Alternatives and Rewrite, Scriba's page, Fontes and Cite.
- On a laptop or desktop, Engine, "Writing model inside this window" can
  install a writing model in the page (about 850 MB, once; it needs
  WebGPU, as in Chrome or Edge). On a phone or tablet the web app offers
  no writing model: the system closes a page that takes that much memory.
- Engine, "Turn on the grammar model" (about 124 MB, once) adds careful
  grammar suggestions. Scriba can find the speakers in a recording (about
  33 MB, once). Both then work offline.


PROBLEMS
--------
- "This site can't be reached", DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN, or a page
  that never finishes loading on your own domain: the domain's records, not
  the files. See YOUR OWN ADDRESS above,
  and open the .netlify.app address meanwhile.
- "Page not found" on Netlify: the folder dropped was not the one with
  index.html at its top. Drop "Inkwell_Web_App_v7p43" itself.
- The address shows "404" on GitHub: wait a few minutes; check that
  index.html is at the top of the repository, not inside a folder.
- The old version still shows: close Inkwell fully and open it again while
  online.
- A model will not start: Engine, "Diagnose this device", and send me the
  report.

INKWELL 7.44 FOR WINDOWS
========================

This zip brings Inkwell 7.44 to the Inkwell already installed on your
Windows computer (from Inkwell-7.37-Windows-Setup.exe). There is no new
installer this time: GitHub Actions, which builds the Windows installer, has
not started since 10 October. The installed program always opens the newest
Inkwell page it finds in its own folder, so 7.44 is one folder dropped in.

INSTALL (ABOUT A MINUTE)
------------------------
1. Close Inkwell if it is open.
2. Unzip: right-click the zip, Extract All, Extract. A window opens with the
   folder "Inkwell 7.44 page" in it.
3. Open Inkwell's own folder: click the address bar at the top of a File
   Explorer window, type   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Inkwell   and press Enter.
   You should see Inkwell.exe there.
4. Drag the folder "Inkwell 7.44 page" into that window.
   If an older folder such as "Inkwell 7.40 page" is there, you can delete
   it: Inkwell always opens the newest page.
5. Open Inkwell from the Start menu as usual. Look for "v7.44" beside the
   date at the top.

Your documents, the Start menu entry and the uninstaller stay as they are.
To go back to 7.37, delete the folder "Inkwell 7.44 page" from Inkwell's
folder.

If Inkwell is not installed on this computer yet: install
Inkwell-7.37-Windows-Setup.exe first (GitHub release "Inkwell 7.37 for
Windows (build 29)", under Assets). If Windows SmartScreen says it protected
your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. Then follow the steps above.

THE MAC MINI'S MODEL ON THIS COMPUTER
-------------------------------------
On the Mac mini: Studio, Engine, "Share with my other devices on this
Wi-Fi". It shows an address and a 6-digit code. Here: Engine, "Another
computer's model". Enter both. The pill at the top then names the Mac mini's
model.

NEW IN 7.44
-----------
- A thesis of 100,000 words opens in about two seconds, and typing stays
  quick (about 40 ms a key, where it would have taken many seconds).
  Checking runs in the background and shows how far it has got. Saved
  versions are kept in the browser's larger store, so a long document is
  safe.
- Tabula searches like Word. Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac, or Home, Find) opens
  the Navigation pane: every result is highlighted and listed with its
  page and the words around it. Enter goes to the next, Shift+Enter to the
  one before, and "Back to where I was" returns you. Replace and Replace
  all can be undone; with Track changes on, they are tracked. Footnotes
  are searched too. The Headings tab shows the outline, with the pages and
  "you are here".
- My way of working (Studio, in Inkwell; View, Comfort, in Tabula; a
  button in Scriba and Fontes): eleven supports, each off until you switch
  it on. Start small, One next step, Draft now fix later, Focus ruler,
  Gentle time, Where was I?, Today so far, Three at a time, Messages wait
  for me, Quiet senses, and Reading comfort (text size, spacing, the clear
  Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, and the tint of the paper). One choice
  serves all four apps.
- A footnote's number is legible in night mode.

NEW IN 7.43
-----------
- Night mode is legible: the selected tab and the other light-gold
  buttons now carry dark letters (white on them measured 1.9 to 1), and
  the blue buttons are a shade deeper. Every button in the desk, the
  dialogs and the three rooms measured 4.5 to 1 or better.
- Tabula shows real pages for a document written line by line (before, it
  showed one long sheet), and headings keep their level both ways.
- Rewrite recasts whole sentences offline, with no model: "It is very
  clear that this is a really important issue" becomes "This is an
  important issue"; "There are many students who struggle" becomes "Many
  students struggle"; "make use of" becomes "use"; "the ways in which"
  becomes "how"; "a big role" becomes "a significant role".
- Every change in a rewrite shows why it was made, offers other ways to
  say it ("Clearly, this is an..."), and has a tick to keep it or keep
  your own words instead. "Try first" lets you find the changes yourself
  from hints, then compare with Inkwell's version.
- A writing model now gives two versions, a fuller one and a lighter one
  closer to your words. It keeps your citations; an answer that only
  describes your passage is not shown; a version that changes who is
  speaking, or strays far from your words, says so.
- The writing mark is out of 100 on the UK scale and read onto UK and US
  grades, undergraduate and postgraduate (the US letters follow the
  AACRAO EDGE table). Choose Writing, or Writing and argument.
- Scholarly writing is no longer marked down: four fifths of the style
  notes on published scholarship were wrong and are gone, and the spelling
  checker knows scholarly vocabulary, names, and the terms you use often.
- The writing model in the browser window loads in Chrome, Edge and
  Safari, not only Firefox, and must answer once before it is called
  ready.
- The side buttons in Tabula, Scriba and Fontes, and in their floating
  windows, no longer have a heavy square shadow.

NEW IN 7.42
-----------
- Inkwell catches more mistakes offline, with no model: the errors people
  make most when writing English (he go, they was, didn't knew, enjoy to
  play, by use, many student, every students, in future, married with, in
  Monday, infront, and the commas left out after "If you want a car" or
  "In my opinion"). Measured on learner essays: half again as many errors
  found, at the same precision; one correct sentence in twenty touched.
- Every note now offers something to do: a fix, a few wordings to try, or
  a question whose answer builds the new sentence ("Who did it?" turns a
  passive sentence active). Notes on words already right are gone, and two
  notes on one place are one. Every note has a lesson of its own kind.
- Clarity and style notes are shown from the start (hide them with one
  button if you want only correctness).
- The web app's tab icon follows the theme: the night seal in dark mode.
- An iPhone or iPad with no app can open Inkwell from the Mac mini itself:
  on the Mac mini, Studio ▸ Engine ▸ Share; on the phone, Safari at
  http://<the address shown>/ and the six-digit code once in Engine.
- The writing model's download (web app) keeps its progress on the pill,
  and a failed download says why in plain words.

NEW IN 7.41
-----------
- The Mac app is small enough to come straight in the chat again (under
  30 MB). The program that runs the models inside the page (Scriba's
  transcription and speakers, the grammar model) is fetched once, the first
  time one of them runs, when that model downloads itself anyway. It is
  checked against its fingerprint and kept, so it then works offline.
- The Mac app has the new seal as its icon.
- The web app shows the seal in the browser tab, and as the picture when you
  send its link.

NEW IN 7.40
-----------
- Inkwell keeps your library safe by itself. There is nothing to export,
  no reminder, and no file on your Desktop. It copies your whole library
  (the desk, Tabula, Fontes, and Scriba's transcripts) when it opens, every
  ten minutes while you work, and when you put it away. File, Earlier
  copies brings back any earlier day, and can itself be undone. If Inkwell
  ever opens with its storage emptied, it puts your library back by itself.
- A file is used only to move your library to another device: File, Move
  the library to another device. The file now carries every room, and
  opening it adds what is new without replacing anything.
- Sticky notes have the 3D look of Carpe Diem and the Forge again (the
  bevel, the pop, and handles on every edge and corner), in Inkwell's own
  shades: Foolscap, Gilt, Lapis, Claret, Laurel and Vespers.
- A computer linked over Wi-Fi that has no writing model loaded now says
  so ("computer linked, no model loaded"). Its simple fallback rules are no
  longer shown as a model's writing.

LIMITS OF THIS ROUTE
--------------------
The 7.41 page runs inside the 7.37 program. What lives in the program, not
the page, stays as in 7.37 until a new Windows build can be made:
- Right-click Paste does not read text copied from other apps; Ctrl+V does.
- Inkwell's earlier copies are kept in its own storage on this computer,
  not also in a folder of their own.
- When this computer shares its own model with other devices, Scriba's
  summaries from those devices need a newer server; share from the Mac mini
  instead.
This route was checked here by running the 7.37 program's own code that
chooses the page; it has not yet been tried on a Windows computer. Tell me
what you see.

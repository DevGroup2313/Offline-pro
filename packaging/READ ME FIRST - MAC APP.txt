INKWELL 7.50 FOR MAC: THE READY-MADE APP
========================================

This zip holds Inkwell.app, ready to use. There is nothing to build, no
Terminal, and no disk image, so no disk icon stays on your Desktop.

WHICH ZIP
---------
  Inkwell-7.45-Mac-Intel.zip          Intel Macs (the 2019 MacBook Pro)
  Inkwell-7.45-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip   M1, M2, M3, M4 and later (the Mac mini)
To check: Apple menu, About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M..." is Apple Silicon.
"Processor: ... Intel" is Intel.

INSTALL OR UPDATE
-----------------
1. Double-click the zip. A folder appears beside it, in Downloads.
2. Open the folder. Drag Inkwell.app onto "Applications" in the Finder
   sidebar.
3. If Finder says an Inkwell already exists there, choose Replace. Your
   documents are not inside the app, so they stay where they are
   (~/Library/Application Support/Inkwell).
4. Open Inkwell from Applications, not from the Downloads folder.
5. Look for "v7.50" beside the date at the top.

THE FIRST OPENING
-----------------
macOS checks every app that does not come from the App Store. Inkwell is not
signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS asks once.

macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:
  1. Open Inkwell. macOS says it could not verify Inkwell. Press Done.
  2. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security.
  3. Beside "Inkwell was blocked", press Open Anyway. Enter your password.
     Press Open.

macOS 14 (Sonoma) and earlier:
  In Applications, hold Control and click Inkwell (or right-click it).
  Choose Open, then Open again.

After this, Inkwell opens normally. macOS may ask again for the microphone
(Scriba's Record) and for the local network (sharing the writing model).
The app has a new seal, so macOS treats it as new. Allow them if you use
those features.

THE MAC MINI'S MODEL ON YOUR OTHER DEVICES
------------------------------------------
On the Mac mini: Studio, Engine, "Share with my other devices on this
Wi-Fi". It shows an address and a 6-digit code. Inkwell must stay open
there.
On the MacBook Pro or a Windows laptop: Engine, "Another computer's model".
On the Fire tablet: Engine, "Your computer's model".
Enter the address and the code. The pill at the top then names the Mac
mini's model. If it says "computer linked, no model loaded", load a model on
the Mac mini first (Engine there).

NEW IN 7.50
-----------
- The corrector catches more, offline and with no model: a preposition
  missing its last word ("in spite all") or the wrong one ("interested
  on"), a missing article ("is experienced person"), a singular after a
  quantity ("many problem"), the verb's form after will, can, does, have
  and "to" ("will delighted", "have went", "a way to learning"),
  agreement ("Sharing rooms have", "the chances of accident is"), a
  capital inside a sentence ("thinking It would"), and slips such as
  "nowaday". Measured against 7.49 on learner essays: about a tenth more
  errors found, precision kept, correct prose touched no more than
  before. Every note carries its lesson.
- Learning as you go: when you accept a fix, one line at the foot of the
  page says the rule behind it, and goes. Once a day, one sentence of
  your own that you mended on an earlier day comes back, to mend from
  memory. Your patterns groups your slips by family. Both are on from the
  start; My way of working switches them off.
- Tabula saves a new document to the device (Word, PDF, text, web page)
  and prints, inside Inkwell, on every device. Inkwell is offered to open
  Word, PDF and text files from the device (Share, Open with, a double
  click), into Tabula.
- Safari: the writing model installs and runs (two faults mended).
- The seal icon fills its canvas on the Mac and on the iPhone and iPad.
- The door offers the guided tour as a quiet link above its buttons; the
  Welcome page's first example gets a proper rewrite; Tabula's side
  buttons are sized to their row.
- Ownership made explicit (LICENCE.txt: no licence is granted, permission
  is personal and revocable, the author alone distributes). The app
  checks its own signature and its own text: Studio, The Inkwell Guide,
  "This copy". See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p50.txt.

NEW IN 7.49
-----------
- Your own dictionary. A word Inkwell does not know (a rare word, a less
  common spelling, a name) offers "+ Add" instead of being marked again
  and again. Added words are known in every document. Studio, Rules, Your
  dictionary lists them and removes one. A note you set aside stays set
  aside; "Show them again" brings the notes back.
- One way throughout the document: when a word is capitalised, hyphenated
  or spelled two ways (or quotation marks, apostrophes, dashes, ellipses,
  number ranges, thousands or units are written two ways), Inkwell offers
  the form you use most.
- Sentence checks with a fix: comma splices; a capital at the head of a
  sentence; days, months, peoples, languages and places.
- Without a model: Coach, Grade against a rubric marks the page, and you
  can mark yourself beside it. Draft with me asks a few questions and joins
  your answers into prose. Instruments, Your patterns shows the slips you
  make most, with practice from your own sentences.
- Performance (click the word count in the footer): counts, reading and
  speaking times, readability. The legend above the suggestions filters
  them by kind. Inclusive language notes offer alternatives.
- Every button, card and tab lifts when you point at it and sinks when you
  press it. The saved time and the fold button sit in the footer's right
  corner. See INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p49.txt.

NEW IN 7.48
-----------
- Footnotes as Word shows them, on the desk and in Tabula: a small raised
  number after the word, and each page's notes at the foot of that page,
  under a short rule, in smaller type. Insert footnote (Ctrl+Alt+F; on a
  Mac, Cmd+Option+F) takes the pen to the foot of the page to write the
  note; deleting the number deletes its note; the notes renumber
  themselves.
- Every Word file holds real Word footnotes (the desk's and Tabula's), and
  a Word file's footnotes come in when it is opened. Copy and paste keep
  footnotes, from Word and Google Docs too, and the desk and Tabula pass
  them to each other.
- The notes of the doctoral checks carry their own tag; Revise corrects or
  rewrites a paragraph, a section or the whole document, change by change.
- Pages are measured in the typeface they are drawn in (since 7.33 they
  were not, and a page could run past its foot). See
  INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p48.txt.

NEW IN 7.47
-----------
- The corrector, measured under a doctoral examiner's eye: on 40 published
  articles, wrong spelling fixes fell from about a hundred to four; of 70
  errors an examiner marks, planted in scholarly sentences, 57 are caught
  (before 30); fewer wrong notes on correct prose. See the web app's
  READ ME or INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p47.txt.
- In the web app, the Engine panel shows the model's install as it happens,
  and a browser without WebGPU gets the way to turn it on.


NEW IN 7.46
-----------
- If Inkwell closes while a model is loading, that model no longer loads
  again by itself the next time Inkwell opens. This guard had not worked
  since 7.34.
- In a browser without WebGPU, the web app runs a smaller writing model
  (LFM2.5 350M) instead of bringing the page down.


NEW IN 7.45
-----------
- The checks a doctoral editor makes, on the whole document, short or
  long: -ise beside -ize (both are British; -ize is Oxford spelling); an
  abbreviation used before it is spelled out, spelled out and never used
  again, or spelled out twice; a citation with no entry in the References,
  a year that disagrees, an entry nobody cites, and the list out of
  alphabetical order; the small marks of citations kept one way; a
  quotation with no page; quotation marks, apostrophes, per cent,
  ellipses, dashes and "e.g.," each written one way; headings, figures,
  tables and chapters numbered in order, and no "see Figure 5" when there
  is no Figure 5.
- Chicago notes at thesis length: placing a footnote takes about a third
  of a second (it took three and a half), and renumbering under a second.
  A note marker followed by a colon now renumbers too. No correction ever
  takes a note marker with it.
- Tabula: a long paragraph runs on to the next page, as in Word, so no
  blank gap is left at the foot of a page.
- British English no longer marks Oxford spelling ("organize") as
  American. The names in your References are known to the spelling check.
- In Safari: the tab's seal follows dark mode, and where the browser has no
  WebGPU the writing model can now run on the processor (slowly; the
  Engine panel says how slowly before you choose it).

NEW IN 7.44
-----------
- A thesis of 100,000 words opens in about two seconds, and typing stays
  quick (about 40 ms a key, where it would have taken many seconds).
  Checking runs in the background and shows how far it has got. Saved
  versions are kept in the browser's larger store, so a long document is
  safe.
- Tabula searches like Word. Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac, or Home, Find) opens
  the Navigation pane: every result is highlighted and listed with its
  page and the words around it. Enter goes to the next, Shift+Enter to the
  one before, and "Back to where I was" returns you. Replace and Replace
  all can be undone; with Track changes on, they are tracked. Footnotes
  are searched too. The Headings tab shows the outline, with the pages and
  "you are here".
- My way of working (Studio, in Inkwell; View, Comfort, in Tabula; a
  button in Scriba and Fontes): eleven supports, each off until you switch
  it on. Start small, One next step, Draft now fix later, Focus ruler,
  Gentle time, Where was I?, Today so far, Three at a time, Messages wait
  for me, Quiet senses, and Reading comfort (text size, spacing, the clear
  Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, and the tint of the paper). One choice
  serves all four apps.
- A footnote's number is legible in night mode.

NEW IN 7.43
-----------
- Night mode is legible: the selected tab and the other light-gold
  buttons now carry dark letters (white on them measured 1.9 to 1), and
  the blue buttons are a shade deeper. Every button in the desk, the
  dialogs and the three rooms measured 4.5 to 1 or better.
- Tabula shows real pages for a document written line by line (before, it
  showed one long sheet), and headings keep their level both ways.
- Rewrite recasts whole sentences offline, with no model: "It is very
  clear that this is a really important issue" becomes "This is an
  important issue"; "There are many students who struggle" becomes "Many
  students struggle"; "make use of" becomes "use"; "the ways in which"
  becomes "how"; "a big role" becomes "a significant role".
- Every change in a rewrite shows why it was made, offers other ways to
  say it ("Clearly, this is an..."), and has a tick to keep it or keep
  your own words instead. "Try first" lets you find the changes yourself
  from hints, then compare with Inkwell's version.
- A writing model now gives two versions, a fuller one and a lighter one
  closer to your words. It keeps your citations; an answer that only
  describes your passage is not shown; a version that changes who is
  speaking, or strays far from your words, says so.
- The writing mark is out of 100 on the UK scale and read onto UK and US
  grades, undergraduate and postgraduate (the US letters follow the
  AACRAO EDGE table). Choose Writing, or Writing and argument.
- Scholarly writing is no longer marked down: four fifths of the style
  notes on published scholarship were wrong and are gone, and the spelling
  checker knows scholarly vocabulary, names, and the terms you use often.
- The writing model in the browser window loads in Chrome, Edge and
  Safari, not only Firefox, and must answer once before it is called
  ready.
- The side buttons in Tabula, Scriba and Fontes, and in their floating
  windows, no longer have a heavy square shadow.

NEW IN 7.42
-----------
- Inkwell catches more mistakes offline, with no model: the errors people
  make most when writing English (he go, they was, didn't knew, enjoy to
  play, by use, many student, every students, in future, married with, in
  Monday, infront, and the commas left out after "If you want a car" or
  "In my opinion"). Measured on learner essays: half again as many errors
  found, at the same precision; one correct sentence in twenty touched.
- Every note now offers something to do: a fix, a few wordings to try, or
  a question whose answer builds the new sentence ("Who did it?" turns a
  passive sentence active). Notes on words already right are gone, and two
  notes on one place are one. Every note has a lesson of its own kind.
- Clarity and style notes are shown from the start (hide them with one
  button if you want only correctness).
- The web app's tab icon follows the theme: the night seal in dark mode.
- An iPhone or iPad with no app can open Inkwell from the Mac mini itself:
  on the Mac mini, Studio ▸ Engine ▸ Share; on the phone, Safari at
  http://<the address shown>/ and the six-digit code once in Engine.
- The writing model's download (web app) keeps its progress on the pill,
  and a failed download says why in plain words.

NEW IN 7.41
-----------
- The Mac app is small enough to come straight in the chat again (under
  30 MB). The program that runs the models inside the page (Scriba's
  transcription and speakers, the grammar model) is fetched once, the first
  time one of them runs, when that model downloads itself anyway. It is
  checked against its fingerprint and kept, so it then works offline.
- The Mac app has the new seal as its icon.
- The web app shows the seal in the browser tab, and as the picture when you

NEW IN 7.40
-----------
- Inkwell keeps your library safe by itself. There is nothing to export,
  no reminder, and no file on your Desktop. It copies your whole library
  (the desk, Tabula, Fontes, and Scriba's transcripts) when it opens, every
  ten minutes while you work, and when you put it away. File, Earlier
  copies brings back any earlier day, and can itself be undone. If Inkwell
  ever opens with its storage emptied, it puts your library back by itself.
- A file is used only to move your library to another device: File, Move
  the library to another device. The file now carries every room, and
  opening it adds what is new without replacing anything.
- Sticky notes have the 3D look of Carpe Diem and the Forge again (the
  bevel, the pop, and handles on every edge and corner), in Inkwell's own
  shades: Foolscap, Gilt, Lapis, Claret, Laurel and Vespers.
- A computer linked over Wi-Fi that has no writing model loaded now says
  so ("computer linked, no model loaded"). Its simple fallback rules are no
  longer shown as a model's writing.
- The Mac app comes as this zip: unzip, drag to Applications.

IF MACOS SAYS "INKWELL IS DAMAGED"
----------------------------------
That message means the app's seal does not match its files. It should not
appear with this zip. If it does, tell me, and do not change your security
settings. As a last resort only, Terminal can clear the download mark:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Inkwell.app

HOW THIS APP WAS MADE, AND ITS LIMITS
-------------------------------------
GitHub Actions, which built the Mac app on a Mac, has not started since
10 October. So this app is the last one Actions built (7.37), with the 7.41
page, rooms, launcher and server put in, and the whole app sealed again
(packaging/make_mac_prebuilt.py). The seal was checked file by file here
(packaging/verify_mac_app.py), but this app has not yet been opened on a
real Mac. The program that runs the writing model (llama.cpp) is the one
from 7.37. Tell me what you see.

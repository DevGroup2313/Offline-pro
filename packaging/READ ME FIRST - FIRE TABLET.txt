INKWELL 7.42 FOR THE FIRE TABLET (ANDROID)
==========================================

THIS PACKAGE HOLDS: Inkwell-7.42-build1.apk, the app itself, built and
signed with Inkwell's own key, so it installs over the Inkwell already on
the tablet and keeps your documents.
NOT FOR: iPhone or iPad (an iPhone or iPad cannot open an .apk file).


INSTALL ON THE FIRE TABLET BY USB
---------------------------------
1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. On the tablet: Settings, Security & Privacy, Apps from Unknown Sources:
   allow the Files app. (You did this once already if Inkwell is on the
   tablet; the wording may differ slightly between Fire OS versions.)
3. Connect the tablet to the computer with its USB cable. If the tablet asks
   what the connection is for, choose "File transfer". Windows shows the
   tablet in File Explorer. A Mac needs a separate file transfer app for
   Android devices.
4. Copy the .apk into the tablet's "Download" folder.
5. On the tablet: open Files, then Downloads, tap the .apk, then Install
   (or Update).
6. Open Inkwell. Look for "v7.42" beside the date at the top.


YOUR DOCUMENTS
--------------
- Installing a new APK over the old one keeps your documents, because every
  build is signed with the same key.
- From 7.40, Inkwell also keeps copies of your whole library by itself, in
  the app's own folder on the tablet: File, Earlier copies.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the tablet, copies included.
  To move documents to another device: File, Move the library to another
  device.


NEW IN 7.42
-----------
- Inkwell catches more mistakes offline, with no model: the errors people
  make most when writing English (he go, they was, didn't knew, enjoy to
  play, by use, many student, every students, in future, married with, in
  Monday, infront, and the commas left out after "If you want a car" or
  "In my opinion"). Measured on learner essays: half again as many errors
  found, at the same precision; one correct sentence in twenty touched.
- Every note now offers something to do: a fix, a few wordings to try, or
  a question whose answer builds the new sentence ("Who did it?" turns a
  passive sentence active). Notes on words already right are gone, and two
  notes on one place are one. Every note has a lesson of its own kind.
- Clarity and style notes are shown from the start (hide them with one
  button if you want only correctness).
- The web app's tab icon follows the theme: the night seal in dark mode.
- An iPhone or iPad with no app can open Inkwell from the Mac mini itself:
  on the Mac mini, Studio ▸ Engine ▸ Share; on the phone, Safari at
  http://<the address shown>/ and the six-digit code once in Engine.
- The writing model's download (web app) keeps its progress on the pill,
  and a failed download says why in plain words.

NEW IN 7.41
-----------
- The Mac app is small enough to come straight in the chat again (under
  30 MB). The program that runs the models inside the page (Scriba's
  transcription and speakers, the grammar model) is fetched once, the first
  time one of them runs, when that model downloads itself anyway. It is
  checked against its fingerprint and kept, so it then works offline.
- The Mac app has the new seal as its icon.
- The web app shows the seal in the browser tab, and as the picture when you
  send its link.

NEW IN 7.40
-----------
- Inkwell keeps your library safe by itself. There is nothing to export,
  no reminder, and no file on your Desktop. It copies your whole library
  (the desk, Tabula, Fontes, and Scriba's transcripts) when it opens, every
  ten minutes while you work, and when you put it away. File, Earlier
  copies brings back any earlier day, and can itself be undone. If Inkwell
  ever opens with its storage emptied, it puts your library back by itself.
- A file is used only to move your library to another device: File, Move
  the library to another device. The file now carries every room, and
  opening it adds what is new without replacing anything.
- Sticky notes have the 3D look of Carpe Diem and the Forge again (the
  bevel, the pop, and handles on every edge and corner), in Inkwell's own
  shades: Foolscap, Gilt, Lapis, Claret, Laurel and Vespers.
- A computer linked over Wi-Fi that has no writing model loaded now says
  so ("computer linked, no model loaded"). Its simple fallback rules are no
  longer shown as a model's writing.


CORRECTIONS AND MODELS ON THIS DEVICE (7.35 AND LATER)
------------------------------------------------------
- Since 7.35 the Fire tablet runs a writing model of its own, offline:
  Engine, "Writing model on this tablet". It is LFM2.5 1.2B Instruct
  (Liquid AI), downloaded once (about 730 MB), then kept on the tablet.
  It runs in a separate process, so if Fire OS needs the memory back it
  closes the model, never your page. Measured behind Inkwell's safety
  filter: 37% of learner sentences improved, 3% made worse, 3% of correct
  sentences changed.
- The tablet's own model comes first everywhere; your laptop's model
  (below) is second, unless you choose it first in Engine.
- Without a model, corrections come from the rules engine, offline; it
  shows only correctness unless you press "Show clarity and style notes".
- Every rewrite is checked: one that drops a citation or number, or adds a
  name, is marked in red with exactly what it changed.
- Cite (Insert, Citation): the same engine and styles Zotero uses (APA,
  Harvard, Chicago, OSCOLA, MHRA, MLA, IEEE, Nature), offline. Adding a
  source by DOI or ISBN needs the internet; BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON do not.

NEW IN 7.36: SPELLING, AND A CAREFUL GRAMMAR MODEL (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- A dictionary spelling checker now runs on this device, offline, with
  suggestions and "Add to dictionary" (switch: Studio, Rules).
- Engine, "Turn on the grammar model" (about 124 MB, once): it reads each
  paragraph as you pause and adds suggestions; nothing changes unless you
  accept. Only its reliable kinds are shown (agreement, articles,
  prepositions, spelling, verb forms). Measured: 80% to 86% of its
  suggestions match a human corrector's; on a 5,486-word thesis it
  suggested nothing.
- Coach menu: an authorship report, and "Grade against a rubric".


NEW IN 7.36: SCRIBA, LIKE CAPTION.ED, OFFLINE
--------------------------------------------
Scriba (the microphone room) now works on a recording the way Caption.Ed
does. Open a recording (or "Import audio or video") and press Transcribe:
- Playback and editing: the transcript follows the sound; a time plays from
  there; every paragraph can be corrected in place; search; exports as
  text, Word, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or straight into Inkwell's document.
- Notes and highlights: Important, Question, Action, Revisit; a note on
  any moment; a scratchpad; slides from a PDF or photos of the board.
- Your words: names and terms spelled your way in every transcript, and a
  switch that removes "um", "uh" and "er".
- Speakers: name them by hand, or press "Find the speakers". This runs on
  the device with two small open models (about 33 MB, fetched once with
  the internet, then offline). Measured on recorded meetings: about 85% of
  speech lands in a paragraph naming the right person, about 95% when the
  speakers are found before transcribing (Scriba then starts a new
  paragraph where the speaker changes). The number of speakers is often
  wrong on short recordings; check the names, and give two speakers the
  same name to join them. Details: tools/speakers/README.md.
- Summarise: a summary with chapters, action items, meeting minutes,
  revision notes with questions, or your own prompt. These come from the
  writing model Inkwell is running on your devices; none is sent anywhere
  else. Any name or number the transcript does not contain is marked so
  you can check it. With the small tablet model the summaries are short
  and plain, and they repeat the speech model's mishearings.


YOUR COMPUTER'S MODEL
---------------------
- Stronger rewriting and drafting come from a computer over the same
  Wi-Fi: the Mac mini, or a laptop. On the computer (Inkwell for Mac or
  Windows): Engine, "Share with my other devices on this Wi-Fi"; it shows
  an address and a 6-digit code. Inkwell must stay open there. On this
  device: Engine, "Your computer's model": enter both, then "Connect to my
  computer".



APPENDIX: BUILDING THE APK YOURSELF ON GITHUB
---------------------------------------------
This is for when GitHub Actions works again (it has not started since
10 October; the APK in this package was built without it). The project is
in the private repository; these were the steps.

PART A. PUT THE PROJECT ON GITHUB (ONCE)
----------------------------------------
1. Sign in at github.com. Top right: "+", then "New repository".
   Name it, for example, inkwell-fire. Choose PRIVATE: this package holds the
   app's signing key (the folder "signing"), which must not be public.
   Leave every other option as it is. Press "Create repository".

2. Unzip this package on your computer. Open the folder inside it.

3. Make hidden files visible, so the folder ".github" can be seen:
     Windows: File Explorer, View, Show, Hidden items.
     Mac: in Finder press Cmd + Shift + full stop.

4. On the new repository's page, click "uploading an existing file".
   Select EVERYTHING inside the package folder (the folders .github, app and
   signing, and every file beside them) and drag it all onto the page.
   Drag the CONTENTS, not the package folder itself.
   Wait until every file is listed, then press "Commit changes".

5. Check the repository's main page. It must show, at the top level:
     .github   app   signing   build.gradle   settings.gradle   gradle.properties
   If "app" sits inside another folder, see "Problems" below: the build still
   works, because it finds the project wherever it is.
   If ".github" is missing, the uploader skipped it (it often does). Then:
     Add file, Create new file. As the name, type exactly:
        .github/workflows/build-apk.yml
     Open "WORKFLOW build-apk.yml (copy).txt" from this package, copy ALL of
     its text, paste it into the big box, and press "Commit changes".


PART B. BUILD THE APK
---------------------
6. Open the "Actions" tab of the repository. A run called
   "Build Inkwell for Android" starts by itself after each commit. If none
   is running, click "Build Inkwell for Android" on the left, then
   "Run workflow", then the green "Run workflow" button.
   If GitHub asks you to enable Actions, enable them.

7. Wait for the green tick (about 2 minutes; the first run can take longer).

8. On the repository's main page, open "Releases" (right-hand side). The
   newest release, "Inkwell 7.41 (build N)", has the APK under "Assets".


PART E. UPDATING INKWELL LATER
------------------------------
- New Inkwell page: upload the new Inkwell.html into app/src/main/assets/
  (replace the old one). If the vendor files changed, upload those into
  app/src/main/assets/vendor/.
- New version number: in app/build.gradle, change versionName '7.41'.
  (versionCode rises by itself with every build, so Android accepts each
  build as an update.)
- New icon: replace the five ic_launcher.png files in app/src/main/res/
  (48, 72, 96, 144 and 192 pixels square). "artwork/inkwell-seal-1024.png" is
  the seal with transparent corners, to make them from.
- Every commit starts a new build; install its APK as in Part C.


PROBLEMS
--------
- No run appears in Actions: the workflow file is missing. Do step 5 again.
- "Directory ... does not contain a Gradle build": the workflow could not find
  settings.gradle. Check that app, build.gradle, settings.gradle and
  gradle.properties were all uploaded, side by side in one folder.
- "No settings.gradle in this repository": the same cause.
- A red step called "Publish a release": in the repository, Settings, Actions,
  General, Workflow permissions: choose "Read and write permissions", Save,
  and run the workflow again.
- Any other red step: open it, read its last lines; the full log is attached
  to the run as "build-log".
- The tablet refuses to install over the old app ("App not installed"): the
  new APK was signed with a different key. Use the same "signing" folder as
  before, or uninstall and install the new APK (uninstalling deletes the documents on
  the tablet, so move them first: File, Move the library to another device).

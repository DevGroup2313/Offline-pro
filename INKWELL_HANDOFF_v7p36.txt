INKWELL v7.36: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 7 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p34.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.35.

Look for "v7.36" beside the date at the top. If it is not there, the device
is not running 7.36.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL
------------------
On every device: File, Export library, and keep the file. Each new build
installs over the old one and keeps your documents, but uninstalling
deletes them. Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package holds the
app's signing key.

WHAT CAME IN 7.35
-----------------
- The Fire tablet runs a writing model of its own (LFM2.5 1.2B Instruct,
  through llama.cpp built into the app), offline, in a separate process.
  It comes first; the laptop's model over the Wi-Fi is second. Tested on an
  Android emulator in CI with the real model; not yet on your Fire tablet.
- Scriba in the web app: stronger speech models (chosen by measurement),
  cuts at pauses, no repeated phrases.

WHAT CAME IN 7.36
-----------------
1. The seal. Cormorant lettering; brass rim on a cream field by day,
   midnight-blue rim by night. It is the app icon on every device (Fire,
   iPhone and iPad, Mac, Windows, web), with the cream field kept so it is
   not see-through on a home screen. On the door and in the header it is a
   physical 3D push button.
2. The house style. An elevated door; physical buttons; night mode in the
   seal's midnight palette.
3. Clepsydra, from Carpe Diem, exactly (its own code and every style rule it
   applies, captured from Carpe Diem). It lives in the toolbar chip that
   replaced "25"; the running time shows in Tabula's hourglass, a click
   stops it, and the panel clears when the timer starts.
4. Schedae: a quiet 44 px token in the house style; it still drags.
5. Scriba, like Caption.Ed, offline, in four stages:
   - Playback and editing: the transcript follows the sound; times play;
     corrections in place; search; text, Word, SRT, WebVTT; into the
     document.
   - Notes and highlights: four highlight kinds, notes on moments, a
     scratchpad, slides from a PDF (pdf.js 3.11.174, Apache 2.0) or images.
   - Speakers and words: your word list and filler removal for every
     transcript; speakers named by hand or found on the device; exports name
     them (WebVTT with its voice tags).
   - Summaries and templates: summary with chapters, action items, minutes,
     revision notes with questions, your own prompt; from the writing model
     Inkwell is running; every answer passes the fact guard.
6. The companion server (Mac and Windows) has a new /api/ask for Scriba's
   summaries. An older server still works through /api/compose.

MEASURED IN 7.36
----------------
Finding the speakers (tools/speakers/README.md has every number):
- AMI meetings, one room microphone, four speakers. Settings chosen on six
  meetings, reported on six others (183.7 minutes).
- DER 18.7%. 84.7% of one-person speech lands in a paragraph naming the
  right person; 94.8% when the speakers are found before transcribing.
- On 3 to 10 minute excerpts the error is similar (18 to 20%), but the
  number of speakers was right in only 7 of 17. Given the number, it was
  right in 15 of 17, at the cost of more speech given to the wrong person
  (DER 26.5%).
- Speed on this build machine: a 3 minute recording took 82 seconds in
  Chromium, including the first download of the models. Not measured on a
  tablet.

Summaries:
- Run end to end in Chromium with LFM2.5 1.2B (the Fire tablet's model)
  through the companion server, on a 3 minute meeting excerpt: all five
  templates answered (2 to 10 seconds each on this machine when it was not
  busy), the fact guard flagged nothing wrongly after the fixes, times in a
  summary play the recording.
- Quality is limited by the small model and by the speech model's
  mishearings: the summaries are short and plain, and a garbled phrase in
  the transcript comes back garbled. No quality score was measured; I have
  no verified benchmark for transcript summaries to report.

STAGE C: GRAMMARLY PARITY, MEASURED (tools/grammar/README.md)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Grammarly itself could not be run here, so there is no side-by-side score.
Inkwell's engines were measured with ERRANT, the standard scorer, on
learner essays (Write & Improve), CoNLL-2014 and correct Wikipedia text;
settings were chosen on one half and reported on data never used.
- Added: a dictionary spelling checker (SCOWL word lists, offline, every
  device) with suggestions, "Add to dictionary" and an on/off switch.
  Inkwell's checking went from F0.5 0.061 to 0.140 on Write & Improve and
  from 0.015 to 0.199 on CoNLL-2014; correct Wikipedia sentences given a
  suggestion went from 1.6% to 3.5%.
- Added: an authorship report (Coach): typed, pasted, model-written,
  suggestions accepted, dictated, inserted; a report to save.
- Added: grade against a rubric (Coach), with quotations checked against
  the page. Small models give top marks too readily; the page says so.
- Fixed: the companion server's model died when two requests overlapped.
- Found: the grammar model GECToR (removed in 7.33 at your request) is the
  strongest corrector measured: with rules and spelling, F0.5 0.498 on
  Write & Improve and 0.576 on CoNLL-2014, against 0.140 and 0.199 without
  it; it would change about 10% of correct Wikipedia sentences (5.6% in the
  7.32 app), many of them real slips. The Fire's writing model corrects less
  well (0.356 and 0.460). It stays removed until you decide.

INKWELL 7.39 FOR MAC
====================

NEW IN 7.39
-----------
- Before you install: in the old Inkwell, File, Export library. Keep the file.
- Alternatives and Rewrite open a card right beside the words you selected
  (on a phone, at the foot of the screen), so you see at once what is
  offered. Nothing changes until you press "Use this"; then Undo shows.
- Alternatives for a word: words to use instead, by meaning, in the word's
  own form ("bigger" gives "larger"). For a sentence: the sentence said
  plainer, more formally and casually, and idioms said three ways ("He lost
  his train of thought": "He lost track of what he was saying", "He lost the
  thread of his argument", "He forgot what he was going to say").
- Rewrite (the new ↻ button, or Rewrite ▾): Scholarly, Professional, Casual,
  Plainer, Concise, Confident, Shorter and Other words, as tabs, each with up
  to three versions and every change with its reason. All of it works with
  no writing model; a model adds its own version.
- The words come from WordNet 3.0 (Princeton University; licence in
  vendor/WORDNET-LICENCE.txt) and a list of common alternatives written for
  Inkwell.

NEW IN 7.38
-----------
- Before you install: in the old Inkwell, File, Export library. Keep the file.
- Rewrite with a prompt now works with no writing model at all. Every
  prompt in the library does something on this device: rules rewrite where
  they can keep the meaning, and each change says what it was and why. For
  what only you can write ("Show, don't tell", "Signpost the argument"), a
  coach points to the exact words, says how, and checks your own try.
- The seals are pressed into the wax; Schedae's button is a quill on
  parchment. The app icons are redrawn.
- Schedae: "Send to Tabula"; six new note colours, by day and by night; each
  colour's name shows when you hover over it, as do the names of the symbol
  buttons everywhere.
- Right-click Paste works with text copied from other apps.
- The installer disk that stayed on the Desktop is put away by itself
  when you open Inkwell from Applications.
- This Mac can use the Mac mini's model: Engine, "Another computer's
  model" (Part D).

New in 7.34: Inkwell for Mac is a ready-made app. It needs no Python, no
pip and no command line tools. Use it on every Mac, old or new.

Why: the older installer built Inkwell from Python on your Mac. On a new
Mac it picked Apple's built-in Python (3.9, with an old pip), which can no
longer install the current window component (pywebview and PyObjC). The
install failed with "update required" messages, and Inkwell opened in the
browser ("the component that draws its own window is not installed").


PART A. INSTALL (OR UPDATE) INKWELL
-----------------------------------
1. If an older Inkwell opens: File, Export library, and keep the file.
   (Your documents also stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Inkwell.)
2. Download the disk image from the GitHub release "Inkwell 7.39 for Mac
   (build N)", under Assets:
     Inkwell-7.39-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg   for M1, M2, M3, M4 and later
                                         (your Mac mini)
     Inkwell-7.39-Mac-Intel.dmg          for Intel Macs (your 2019 MacBook Pro)
   Not sure? Apple menu, About This Mac: "Chip" means Apple Silicon,
   "Processor ... Intel" means Intel.
3. Open the .dmg. Drag Inkwell onto Applications. If asked, Replace.
4. Open Inkwell from Applications. The first time, macOS refuses ("Apple
   could not verify..."), because the app is not signed with a paid Apple
   certificate. Then: System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   press "Open Anyway" beside Inkwell, and confirm. Only once.
   The disk "Inkwell" that the .dmg put on your Desktop is only the
   installer; your documents are never on it. From 7.38, Inkwell puts it
   away by itself when you open Inkwell from Applications. To put it away
   by hand: right-click it on the Desktop and choose Eject.
5. Look for "v7.39" beside the date at the top. If your documents are not
   there: File, Import library, and pick the file from step 1.
6. Scriba: the first time you press Record, macOS asks for the microphone;
   choose Allow.


PART B. THE WRITING MODEL ON THIS MAC
-------------------------------------
Engine, "Writing model on this computer": the panel marks the model
recommended for this Mac's memory; press Install. It downloads the
model once (a few gigabytes) and runs it on this Mac, offline, with Apple's
Metal graphics acceleration on Apple Silicon. Correct, Rewrite and Draft
use it; your tablets use it over the Wi-Fi when sharing is on (Part D).


PART C. BUILD IT YOURSELF (OPTIONAL)
------------------------------------
On GitHub: upload everything in this package to a new repository; the
workflow "Build Inkwell for Mac" (.github/workflows/build-mac.yml, also as
"WORKFLOW build-mac.yml (copy).txt") makes both disk images; see the
iPhone package's READ ME for the upload steps, which are the same.
On this Mac without GitHub: the folder "Build on this Mac (Python)" holds
the older installer. In 7.34 it chooses the newest Python on the Mac and
updates pip first, but it still depends on Python; the .dmg does not.

PART D. YOUR OTHER DEVICES CAN USE THIS COMPUTER'S MODEL, AND THIS ONE ANOTHER'S
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Inkwell here. Engine, under "Sharing this computer's model":
   press "Share with my other devices on this Wi-Fi". It shows an address
   and a code. Keep Inkwell open while others use it.
2. If the computer asks whether to allow Inkwell on the network, choose
   Allow.
3. On the Fire tablet, iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi-Fi: Engine, "Your
   computer's model": type the address and the code, then "Connect to my
   computer".
4. New in 7.38, the other way round: this computer can use a stronger
   one's model, such as the Mac mini's. Engine, "Another computer's
   model": type that computer's address and code, then "Use that
   computer's model". When it is asleep or closed, this computer's own
   model stands in until it answers again.
Only the writing and voice tools are shared, and only with the code. Your
documents are never reachable from the network. "Stop sharing" closes it again.

INKWELL 7.29 ON IPHONE AND IPAD (THE APP, INSTALLED BY USB)
=========================================================
This folder (Inkwell-iOS) is the iPhone and iPad app. GitHub builds it on one
of its own Macs, which has Xcode, so your Mac does not need Xcode. Apple only
lets an app run if it is signed with an Apple ID, so the build is signed on
your Windows computer by Sideloadly as it installs the app over USB.

Please check these steps against Sideloadly's own current instructions: they
were written without access to an iPhone, an iPad or Sideloadly.

A. GET THE APP FILE
   1. On the repository's Releases page, open the newest
      "Inkwell ... for iPhone and iPad" release.
   2. Download the .ipa file under Assets.

B. ONCE, ON THE WINDOWS COMPUTER
   3. Install Sideloadly from its official site (sideloadly.io).
   4. Sideloadly needs Apple's iTunes and iCloud for Windows. Its site says
      which versions (at the time of writing, the ones from Apple's website
      rather than the Microsoft Store).

C. INSTALL
   5. Connect the iPhone or iPad with its cable. Unlock it and tap Trust if
      it asks whether to trust this computer.
   6. Open Sideloadly. Drag the .ipa into it. Choose the device, enter your
      Apple ID, and press Start.
   7. On the device, the first time only:
        Settings, General, VPN & Device Management: tap your Apple ID, then
        Trust.
        Settings, Privacy & Security, Developer Mode: switch it on and let
        the device restart (asked on iOS 16 and later).
   8. Inkwell is now on the Home Screen with the seal. iPhone and iPad
      always draw app icons as rounded squares, so the seal sits on white.

D. EVERY 7 DAYS (FREE APPLE ID)
   With a free Apple ID, Apple stops the app from opening 7 days after it was
   signed. Repeat step 6 with the same .ipa (or a newer one) before then.
   Installing over the existing app should keep your documents, as the app's
   storage belongs to its identifier (studio.inkwell.app), but export your
   library first anyway until you have seen this work on your own device.
   A paid Apple Developer account makes each install last a year instead.

E. YOUR DOCUMENTS
   The app keeps its own library, separate from Safari and from the web app.
   To bring documents across: in the old place, File, Export library; in the
   app, File, Import library. Saving a file opens Apple's "Save to Files"
   dialog.
   NEVER delete the app without File, Export library first: deleting the app
   deletes its storage.

F. SCRIBA
   The first time Scriba records, iOS asks for the microphone: choose Allow.
   On-device Whisper downloads its model the first time, so be online for
   the first recording.

G. IF THE BUILD FAILS
   Open the failed run in Actions ("Build Inkwell for iPhone and iPad"), open
   the red step, and copy its last lines. The build log is also attached to
   the run as "ios-build-log".

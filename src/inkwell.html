Inkwell
An offline writing studio

Inkwell is an ink pot and a workbench at once: it corrects as you write, offers stronger words from its own bank, learns only your voice, and shows its working. Behind this door your documents, your notes, and your rules wait exactly where you left them; every mark it makes stays on this device.

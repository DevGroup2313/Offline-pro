INKWELL v7.42: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 16 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p40.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.41 (7.41 had no notes of its own: the Mac app under
30 MB through the fetch-once model runtime, the seal in every tab and
shared link, and the honest "no model loaded" pill).

Look for "v7.42" beside the date at the top. If it is not there, the device
is not running 7.42.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's
signing key. The measurement data (Write & Improve, LOCNESS, CoNLL-2014,
JFLEG) is never in the repository and never trained on.

WHAT CAME IN 7.42
-----------------
1. G1, the note refiner (src/lib/refine_core.js, INKREF; tests
   tools/rewrite/refine_test.js, 83). The rules engine's notes pass through
   it last, in inkRules after the spelling checker. A note on words already
   right goes (a stative "was born", a repeated name, the reminders that a
   word is correct, which were also removed from the rule packs). A note
   with nothing to choose gets a fix, a few wordings to try ("Try:" chips,
   e.tryAlts and e.altView) or a question whose answer builds the new
   wording (e.ask; inkAskBuild in the page; INKREF.build): "Who did it?"
   turns an agentless passive active with the verb and the number agreed;
   "This what?" names the noun; "Whose work?" puts the citation in. Two
   notes on one place become the more useful one (score: fix > choices >
   advice; grammar over clarity over style); two overlapping fixes never
   both stay fixes (Fix all would corrupt the text). Every kind of note has
   a lesson of its own (INKREF.lesson, read by lessonFor before the general
   fallback). Clarity and style notes are shown by default (inkStyleDefault,
   one-time key ink_style742). Rewrite applies the certain grammar and
   spelling fixes before any style. Measured on the G1 baseline texts:
   advice-only notes 68% to 27% (Wikipedia), 20% to 2% (learners); general
   lessons 14 to 1 and 80 to 19; overlapping notes 36 to 0.
2. G2, the learner-error checks (src/lib/learner_core.js, INKLRN; word
   lists src/lib/learner_data.js from tools/grammar/learner_lists.mjs; tests
   tools/grammar/learner_test.js, 91). Called from ruleEdits as
   learnerCheck, before the refiner. Nine families (sva, form, aux, tense,
   num, art, prep, orth, comma), each tagged e.fam. The numbers and what the
   rules still cannot reach are in tools/grammar/README.md ("7.42"). On the
   held-out learner half: 148 true fixes to 212, false 69 to 119 (precision
   68% to 64%), correct sentences touched 3.7% to 5.1%. The measurement
   runner is tools/grammar/runpage.mjs (the real page in Chromium).
3. The web app's tab icon follows the theme (inkTabIcon in applyMode; the
   site's icon links carry data-ink-day and data-ink-night;
   icons/favicon-32-night.png and icon-192-night.png from the seal's night
   face; no "shortcut icon" link any more). The theme colour follows too.
4. The writing model's download (web app, LFM2.5 1.2B over WebGPU) keeps
   its progress on the pill (window.__inkInstalling; setPill stands back),
   shows "x MB of about 850 MB", and a failed download names huggingface.co
   and says that trying again continues. In this sandbox the browser could
   not reach huggingface.co (the proxy's certificate), so a full load was
   not seen here; the chain up to the download was.
5. The Mac mini serves Inkwell itself to an iPhone or iPad (server.py,
   LanHandler._page: "/", "/Inkwell.html" and "/vendor/..." only, from
   INKWELL_PAGE, set by the launcher from find_app_html, else a page beside
   server.py; nothing else, no climbing paths, the endpoints still need the
   code). The page opened from such an address takes it as its server
   (inkLanOrigin, inkDefaultServer) and asks for the code once; the sharing
   panel says how. Checked in the browser here with a .local stand-in; not
   yet tried on a Mac or an iPhone. The library of that page lives in that
   address's own storage (File, Move the library carries it across).

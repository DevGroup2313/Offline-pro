INKWELL v7.48: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 18 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p47.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.48.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's
signing key. The measurement data (Write & Improve, LOCNESS, CoNLL-2014,
JFLEG, the published articles, the thesis sample, the 100,000-word test
thesis) is never in the repository and never trained on.

WHY 7.48
--------
The writer: footnotes should look as Word shows them, a small number to the
right of the word and the notes with their text at the foot of the page;
the same in Tabula; the same in every Word file, and through copy and
paste. The writer saw the screenshots and approved them before this was
committed. 7.47 was not released on its own; its work (the Engine panel's
install, the corrector under an examiner's eye) is released with 7.48.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. The desk (src/inkwell.html, the flow editor). The text keeps its notes as
   before ([^n] in the text, "[^n]: ..." under # Notes), so every check,
   export and device reads them as it did; only the sheets show them
   otherwise (_fNotes, _fSegHTML, _fSheetNotes):
   - the marker a raised number (its brackets are in the page, hidden, so
     every place in the text is where it was); the definitions and their
     # Notes heading hidden; a definition with no marker stays in sight;
   - each page's notes at its foot (.pgnotes): a short rule, then each note
     with its number raised, smaller type, single spaced, *titles* in
     italics; a page holds less text by its notes' height (_fMeasure counts
     them; a note longer than a page lets the sheet grow);
   - the pen never stands inside a marker or in hidden text (_fSnap,
     _fCaretOK); Backspace after a number deletes the note with it, and the
     notes are numbered again (_fUserEdit; undo brings it back); a selection
     deleted across the hidden notes keeps the notes of markers it keeps;
   - Insert footnote (and Ctrl+Alt+F, Cmd+Option+F) raises the number at the
     pen and takes the pen to the foot of the page; a click on a note writes
     in it; Enter or Esc, or a click in the text, ends it; a click on the
     note's number goes back to the text; a double-click on a number in the
     text opens its note. The 7.45 note card is gone.
   - a suggestion card that points into a note opens the note, its words
     selected; the Outline leaves out the hidden Notes.
   Faults found on the way, both since 7.33: the page's words sit in an
   inline span (.pgw) that takes the general typeface, while the probe that
   measures pages had the serif; and a line break that ends a span opens one
   more line. Pages were measured in one face and drawn in another and ran
   past their foot. The probe now holds the same span, and a page's closing
   line breaks are set after it (_fTailAt). The cut is looked for within
   what fits (it took one line more when a page ended in a blank line). The
   Reading comfort typeface now reaches the words (it stopped at the page).
   Speed, measured on the 100,000-word thesis with 998 notes (390 pages):
   opening 1.4 s (7.47: 2.0 s, without notes at the feet); a fresh layout
   1.9 s (7.47: 2.3 s); a key about 50 ms (7.47: 31 ms). The page is laid
   out once a little past its break and the break read from its lines
   (_fRectBest), confirmed by two measurements; the probes stand in a box
   closed to the page (contain). The pages match a fresh layout after
   every kind of edit; no page runs past its foot.
2. Tabula (src/rooms/tabula.html). The notes stay in #footnotes (saved,
   sent, exported; shown at the end in print), hidden on the screen, and
   each page shows its notes at its foot (#cd-fnpages, fnPrep, fnDraw): the
   pagination leaves their room (a line that cites a note goes on with it);
   a note is written where it stands; Insert footnote takes the pen to the
   foot (no more prompt box); the number in the text is set as Word sets
   it, in the text's colour, without opening its line.
3. Word files.
   - The desk's .docx: real footnotes (footnotes.xml, Word's Footnote Text
     and Footnote Reference styles, the two separators named in
     settings.xml); the # Notes section leaves the body. Until now the
     numbers were raised digits and the notes a list at the end.
   - Tabula's Save as .docx (cdBuildDocx) wrote each number as a plain
     digit and left the notes out; it now writes footnotes and endnotes as
     Word's own, with their italics; the document's type is Times New Roman
     12 pt at 1.7 lines, as the screen shows it (it had no defaults). The
     other writer (buildDocx, the ribbon's Save .docx) keeps note formatting
     and names the separators in settings.xml (they stood in sectPr, where
     the schema has no place for them).
   - Opening a .docx on the desk reads its footnotes and endnotes (they were
     dropped). Zip entries are found through the central directory: files
     that write sizes after the data (LibreOffice) were read as empty, and
     the inflate looped for ever on an empty entry. Zip dates are real.
   Checked by rendering in LibreOffice 24.2 (installed here for it): the
   notes stand at each page's foot under the separator. Not opened in Word.
4. Copy and paste (src/lib/fnclip_core.js, INKFNCLIP, in the desk and in
   Tabula). A selection with notes is copied with them: HTML in Word's own
   footnote form (mso-footnote-id, mso-element:footnote), plain text as the
   desk keeps notes. A paste reads notes from that form, from LibreOffice
   (sdfootnote), from Google Docs (ftnt) and from any page whose reference
   and note link to each other; they take fresh numbers in place. The desk
   and Tabula hand notes to each other (a hidden data-ink-notes box after
   the text), and Tabula's autosave keeps them.
   Tested in Chromium with synthetic clipboard events. Not tested: pasting
   into Word (Word is not here; LibreOffice does not read Word's footnote
   HTML, so it could not stand in), Safari, Firefox.
5. The two designs shown with 7.45 and kept outside the repository until
   now, approved by the writer: the notes of the doctoral checks carry
   their own tag, with no strike-through on the words of a note that
   changes nothing; and Revise (Rewrite, Revise) corrects or rewrites a
   paragraph, a section or the whole document, change by change
   (src/lib/revise_core.js, tools/revise/revise_test.js). The third design
   of that set, the note card, is replaced by the notes at the page foot.

NOT DONE YET
------------
- Corrections inside a note are listed and open the note, but are not
  underlined at the page foot.
- A note longer than the room left is not continued on the next page, as
  Word does; the sheet grows instead (desk) or the line moves on (Tabula).
- Tabula: find does not show hits inside the notes at the page foot;
  endnotes stay at the end (as in Word) and still ask for their text in a
  box.

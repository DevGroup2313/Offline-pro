INKWELL v7.39: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 11 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p38.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.39.

Look for "v7.39" beside the date at the top. If it is not there, the device
is not running 7.39.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL
------------------
On every device: File, Export library, and keep the file. Each new build
installs over the old one and keeps your documents, but uninstalling
deletes them. Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package holds the
app's signing key.

WHAT CAME IN 7.39
-----------------
1. Results beside the selection. Alternatives (✨), Rewrite (the new ↻ in
   the selection bubble, and Rewrite ▾) and Rewrite with a prompt (✍) open a
   card next to the selected words; on a phone, a sheet at the foot of the
   screen. Before, results were added to the side panel under the
   suggestions, where they could be out of sight, so it looked as if
   nothing had happened. Nothing changes until "Use this"; then Undo shows.
   Esc, ✕ or a click outside puts the card away.
2. Alternatives for a word: "Words to use instead", by meaning, each put
   into the word's form ("showed" gives "demonstrated"; "bigger" gives
   "larger"), from a list of common alternatives written for Inkwell (about
   900 words and phrases, src/lib/synonyms_core.js); then WordNet's
   meanings, each with its definition, for the writer to choose the one
   meant (src/lib/lexicon_core.js, vendor/wordnet-lex.txt).
3. Alternatives for a sentence: the sentence plainer, more formal, casual or
   reordered, and idioms said three ways (73 idioms: "He lost his train of
   thought" gives "He lost track of what he was saying", "He lost the thread
   of his argument", "He forgot what he was going to say"), keeping the
   tense and the pronoun. Below them, "Or change one word".
4. Rewrite in a style, as tabs: Scholarly, Professional, Casual, Plainer,
   Concise, Confident, Shorter, Other words. Up to three versions each
   (light touch, fuller, reordered), every change with its reason. The
   method, step by step (src/lib/paraphrase_core.js): idioms in the
   register; 89 hand-checked word pairs, each used only where the
   words around it allow ("try to" and "attempt to"; "set up a company" and
   "establish a company", never "set up the camera"); sentence openers;
   contractions for Casual; one change of order where the meaning holds;
   then the style's rules. Names, quotations and citations are never
   touched. With a writing model, the model adds its version; for
   Alternatives it is asked for three (plainer, more formal, other words),
   each through the fact guard.
5. The web app package is the site itself: unzip, drag the folder into
   Netlify. Its guide now explains connecting your own domain.
6. Fixed on the way: Scholarly made "being shown to children" into "being
   demonstrated to children"; Simplify made "the sufficient conditions"
   into "the enough conditions".

THE WEBSITE ERROR (carpediemofflinestudycompanion.com)
------------------------------------------------------
"This site can't be reached ... DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN" was not caused
by Inkwell's files (no file names that address). Checked on 11 October
through Google's and Cloudflare's public DNS:
- the domain has two A records: 75.2.60.5 (Netlify) and 192.168.0.58, a
  home network address no one outside that home can reach; www has the
  same two;
- Netlify answers at 75.2.60.5 but shows only its own *.netlify.app
  certificate, so no certificate has been issued for the domain.
The remedy is in the web app guide, "YOUR OWN ADDRESS": delete the
192.168.0.58 records at Namecheap, point @ and www to Netlify as Netlify's
guide says, and let Netlify issue the certificate. Meanwhile, the
.netlify.app address works. The domain's name suggests it was set up for
the Carpe Diem study companion: connecting it to Inkwell would take it away
from that site; each project wants its own Netlify site and address.

MEASURED IN 7.39
----------------
- Read by hand, every change on 600 Wikipedia sentences (151 changes, four
  styles) and 600 sentences of native student essays (LOCNESS; 339
  changes): 15 of the 339 were wrong (4.4%) and were fixed, with the
  Wikipedia faults. Then on sentences not seen while fixing (383 LOCNESS
  sentences and 89 thesis sentences): 8 of 317 changes were wrong (2.5%),
  also fixed. The next unseen text may well show a rate near 2.5%, not 0.
- How often a style has something to offer (unseen student essays /
  Wikipedia): Scholarly 23% / 7% of sentences; Casual 36% / 13%; Plainer
  15% / 8%; a sentence's Alternatives 51% / 19%; "Or change one word" 91% /
  77%. The rules change only what they can keep; much text is left as it
  is, and the card says so.
- Word alternatives: the common list covers about half of the content
  words in those texts (most of the rest are subject words such as
  "computer"). WordNet's meanings come after it; for a word with many
  meanings the first is not always the one meant, so each shows its
  definition.
- Tests: tools/rewrite/para_test.js 44 of 44, run.js 230 of 230,
  coach_test.js 38 of 38; the browser test of the card at laptop, iPad and
  phone sizes, by day and by night.
- Grammar figures unchanged from 7.38 (no grammar rule changed).

LIMITS, PLAINLY
---------------
- Offline, Inkwell does not write new sentences. It says idioms and words
  again in another register, reorders where it is safe, and coaches the
  rest. Free paraphrase ("in other words" for any sentence) needs a writing
  model.
- WordNet 3.0 is from 2006 and American: newer senses are missing, and its
  spellings are American ("recognized"). Inkwell's own list avoids words
  spelt differently in British and American English.
- The lexicon takes about 35 MB of memory once read; it is read the first
  time Alternatives or Rewrite is used, not at start.

NOT TESTED ON YOUR DEVICES

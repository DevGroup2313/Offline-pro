INKWELL v7.45: HANDOFF NOTES
============================
Date: 18 October 2026. Read INKWELL_HANDOFF_v7p44.txt and the earlier notes
for everything before 7.45.

Keep this repository PRIVATE: the Fire package's build kit holds the app's

WHAT IS IN 7.45
---------------
1. GAP1, Tabula's pages (committed f92c416): a paragraph runs on to the
   next page as in Word, so a long paragraph no longer leaves a blank gap at
   the foot of a page. A style rule shapes each paragraph's lines around the
   page break; the document is never touched. 100,000 words: 272 pages (306
   before), laid out in about 0.3 s; page breaks and section breaks kept.
2. G3c, the checks a doctoral editor makes (src/lib/dphil_core.js,
   INKDPHIL; tools/dphil/dphil_test.js, 94). They read the whole document
   once, short or long, and add notes where no other note stands:
   - -ise beside -ize (both British; -ize is Oxford spelling), names spared;
   - an abbreviation used before it is spelled out, spelled out and never
     used again, or spelled out twice in one chapter;
   - a citation with no entry in the References, a year that disagrees, an
     entry of a References list cited nowhere (not a Bibliography), the
     list out of alphabetical order;
   - the citations' small marks one way: "(Smith, 2019)" or "(Smith 2019)",
     "p. 4", ": 4" or ", 4", "&" or "and";
   - a quotation of a sentence or more with no page;
   - quotation marks and apostrophes, per cent, ellipses, dashes, "e.g.,":
     each written one way;
   - headings, figures and tables numbered in order; "see Figure 5" past
     the last figure; "see Chapter 7" in a thesis of six chapters;
     "Figure 2" beside "figure 2".
   Casual writing gets none; creative writing only the marks. Measured on
   the 40 published articles, the thesis sample and the 100,000-word thesis
   (read note by note; false alarms found and removed: "expertise",
   official names such as the World Health Organization, quoted titles
   before a year, the chapters of a book under discussion, titles and TV
   series before a year). About 30 ms for the 100,000 words.
   Also: the British pack no longer marks Oxford -ize as American ("analyze"
   still is); names in the document's own References are known to the
   spelling check ("Toombs" was offered "Tombs").
3. CITE1, first part: Chicago notes at thesis length. Measured on the
   100,000-word thesis with 998 notes (Chicago full notes, short notes,
   Ibid., a Bibliography):
   - placing a footnote took 3.5 s, now 0.3 s; renumbering 3.2 s, now
     0.7 s. The page layout keeps every page whose text is unchanged,
     wherever it now begins (until now every page between the marker and
     its note was measured again), and a page whose text changed only in
     its figures is measured at its old fit; the pages are the same as a
     fresh layout after each kind of edit (368 pages compared).
   - a long document is checked a paragraph at a time with its markers
     taken out, and each note is a paragraph of its own, so a new number
     sends nothing to be checked again; a correction never takes a marker
     with it.
   - a marker followed by a colon in the text ("three reasons[^4]: first")
     was read as a note's definition and kept its old number on renumbering;
     fixed.
   - no false notes left in the Notes and the Bibliography (there were
     three: "2nd rev. ed." read as a sentence end, the Notes counted as one
     paragraph of 7,004 words, and 118 author names in notes marked as
     misspelt once each note was read alone).
   - the offline rewrites keep every marker (64 versions of four passages
     with markers); the model's rewrites already pass the fact guard, which
     counts markers.
   The first check of a whole thesis, in the background, takes about 40 s
   here.

4. Safari (committed 057352e). The tab's seal: the page chose the night
   icon for a dark page, then a later boot step added a day seal after it,
   which the browser used; the seal is now drawn only where the page names
   no icon of its own, in the mode in force, and the web app's page also
   names night icons for a device in dark mode (media). The writing model:
   with no WebGPU (the Safari of the writer's diagnostic) it was not
   installed at all; it now runs on the processor with WebAssembly, from the
   q8 files (the q4 files use GatherBlockQuantized, which ONNX Runtime has
   only for WebGPU), named "model_q8" (Transformers.js would look for
   "model_quantized"). Measured in Chromium with WebGPU removed: one
   sentence took 173 s on one thread, 84 s on three. The Engine panel says
   so before the download (about 1.8 GB). Up to eight cores where the page
   is cross-origin isolated; the web app's site does not yet send the
   headers for that (Scriba loads its speech engine from jsDelivr, which
   the headers could stop), so there it runs on one thread.

AFTER THE 7.45 RELEASE
----------------------
- The Mac Intel build stopped at "Make the disk image": hdiutil said
  "Resource busy" (Inwell-Mac-Intel, run 4). The same workflow passed on
  the other runners. The step now detaches anything left mounted, waits
  and tries again, up to five times (.github/workflows/mac.yml).

SHOWN, WAITING FOR THE WRITER'S APPROVAL (not in 7.45)
--------------------------------------------------------
- The note card: a footnote written beside its marker, numbered in its
  place at once; the card opens to be read when the cursor rests on a
  marker.
- Notes of the doctoral checks with their own tag, and no strike-through
  on the words of a note that changes nothing.
- Revise (Rewrite, Revise): a paragraph, a section or the whole document
  corrected or rewritten, change by change (src/lib/revise_core.js,
  tools/revise/revise_test.js, 20).
These are kept outside the repository until approved.
